Title: JOURNEY_MAP keeps direct trainId routes when interlocking routes are added (Bun)
Given: The Bun Station Master (server.ts) has a JOURNEY_MAP with at least one {interlockingId, path} route object
When: you add or change JOURNEY_MAP entries or their dispatch in server.ts
Then: JOURNEY_MAP MUST accept both a direct trainId string and an {interlockingId, path} object, and existing direct `JOURNEY_MAP[action] = "<trainId>"` mappings MUST stay valid.
Prefer:
- Mixed map shape: `{ <direct_action>: "train:<domain>:<train-name>", <interlocked_action>: { interlockingId: "...", path: "...yaml" } }`
Avoid:
- Converting, removing or rejecting existing direct trainId string mappings when you add interlocking routes
Unless:
- JOURNEY_MAP declares no interlocking route object: a pure direct-train Station Master carries no interlocking obligation
Why: The interlocking mapping is additive, so the existing direct mapping remains valid. TrainRunner executes the train in both cases; runner-delegation enforces that.
