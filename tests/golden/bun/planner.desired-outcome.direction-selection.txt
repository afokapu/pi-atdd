Title: Pick the WMBT direction verb from the controlled palette
Given: A WMBT is being authored or edited and needs an optimization direction.
When: The planner chooses the direction verb for the WMBT.
Then: Select the optimization direction verb that matches the WMBT's goal from the controlled palette (minimize, maximize, increase, decrease).
Prefer:
- Use the palette (canonical direction -> synonyms) to map goal wording: minimize ← reduce, decrease, lower, shrink; maximize ← increase, boost, raise, grow; increase ← improve, elevate, accelerate; decrease ← cut, dampen, slow, limit.
- Write a canonical direction (a palette key) rather than a synonym.
- Note: 'increase' and 'decrease' are listed both as canonical keys and as synonyms (of maximize and minimize respectively); the palette does not say which reading wins, so both are palette-conformant—pick the one whose sense matches the goal and do not claim the palette settles it.
Avoid:
- Inventing a direction verb outside the palette.
- Writing a non-key synonym in place of a canonical direction.
- Choosing a direction whose sense contradicts the goal (e.g. minimize for something the goal wants to grow).
Why: The palette is a controlled vocabulary mapping each canonical direction to its synonyms; choosing from it keeps the direction verb within that vocabulary and matched to the goal.
