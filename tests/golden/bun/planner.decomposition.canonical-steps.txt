Title: Name job steps with the canonical step vocabulary, by reference to steps.convention
Given: Job steps are being named, or this protocol's reference to the step vocabulary is being edited
When: you assign a label to a job step or change how this protocol points at the canonical steps
Then: Label every job step with one of the canonical steps owned by steps.convention (Define, Locate, Prepare, Confirm, Execute, Monitor, Modify, Resolve, Conclude), taking the vocabulary by reference to steps.convention.
Prefer:
- Use the exact canonical step names: Define, Locate, Prepare, Confirm, Execute, Monitor, Modify, Resolve, Conclude
- In this protocol, point at steps.convention by reference for what the steps are and mean
Avoid:
- Inventing synonyms or project-specific step names in place of the canonical steps
- Re-enumerating or redefining the steps inside this protocol (e.g. adding its own list of step meanings, extra steps, or changed definitions); copying steps.convention content here is drift
Why: steps.convention owns the step vocabulary, so one shared set of step names keeps job steps consistent; this protocol references that owner instead of copying or redefining it, because copies drift. Using a step name as a label is referencing; restating what the steps are is copying.
