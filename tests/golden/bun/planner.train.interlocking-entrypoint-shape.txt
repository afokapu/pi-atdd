Title: Declare each interlocking's entrypoint shape: exposed flag plus actions or reason
Given: A plan contains an interlocking.
When: The plan is validated in CONFIRM. Every interlocking in the plan is checked, including unchanged ones.
Then: Every interlocking declares whether it is runtime-exposed. exposed:true must list at least one Station Master action name expected to reach it. exposed:false must give a reason.
Prefer:
- Set exposed explicitly on every interlocking.
- List actions by their Station Master action names, meaning the actions expected to reach the interlocking.
Avoid:
- exposed:true with an empty or missing actions list.
- exposed:false with no reason, or with an empty or placeholder reason that only fills the field.
- Putting train IDs in actions instead of Station Master action names.
- Treating a passing result from this check as proof of runtime reachability. The planner validates shape only.
Why: Each interlocking must state whether it is runtime-exposed and which Station Master actions are expected to reach it. That keeps its entrypoint shape explicit and checkable.
