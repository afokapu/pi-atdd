Title: Each wagon procedure step declares its pre-conditions and outputs
Given: A document contains a wagon procedure written as steps
When: the step list is written, or any step is added, removed, moved, renumbered or edited
Then: Every step declares its pre-conditions (what it requires before running) and its outputs (what it produces after).
Avoid:
- Steps whose pre-conditions or outputs are missing or unclear.
Why: Ordered, numbered steps with pre-conditions and outputs make a wagon procedure reproducible and checkable.
