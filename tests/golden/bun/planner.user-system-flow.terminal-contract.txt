Title: Declare exactly one primary artifact as a train's terminal contract
Given: A train exists or is being defined, wherever its definition is stored.
When: The train's terminal step artifact or its family is defined, changed, or checked (either one alone is enough).
Then: The train's terminal step declares exactly one primary artifact, which is the train's terminal contract.
Prefer:
- Name one primary artifact on the final step and treat it as the terminal contract: the source of truth for the train's family.
- Derive the family from the terminal contract: set family = delivery only if the terminal contract BOTH requires an Acceptance Authority commit AND terminates in platform:acceptance:commit-receipt; in every other case set family = behavior.
- Whenever either the terminal artifact or the family changes alone, re-apply the derivation above and make the declared family match its result.
Avoid:
- Declaring zero or several primary artifacts on the terminal step.
- Deriving, guessing or asserting the family from the train id (or its name/prefix) — never do this; only the terminal contract decides.
- Declaring delivery when only one of the two delivery conditions holds.
Why: One declared terminal contract is the single unambiguous source of truth for what a train delivers, so its family is derived from it and never from the train id.
