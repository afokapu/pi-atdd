Title: Recognize and phrase each JTBD step through its step's slotted templates
Given: A planning artifact contains, or is to contain, a JTBD job-step statement
When: The planner recognizes/classifies that statement's step, or writes or rewords it
Then: Recognize and phrase the statement through one of the fixed slotted templates of a single step: its template determines its step, and its slots bind the step verb to concrete objects, constraints or outcomes.
Prefer:
- define: Define {object} as {specification}; Plan {approach} for {outcome}; Select {option} from {alternatives}; Determine {criteria} for {decision}
- locate: Locate {resource} within {constraint}; Gather {inputs} from {sources}; Access {data} via {method}; Retrieve {information} in {timeframe}
- prepare: Prepare {environment} for {activity}; Set up {infrastructure} with {configuration}; Organize {resources} by {criteria}; Examine {inputs} for {validation}
- confirm: Confirm {precondition} before {action}; Validate {input} against {criteria}; Prioritize {items} by {importance}; Decide {path} based on {conditions}
- execute: Execute {action} within {constraint}; Perform {operation} on {target}; Transact {change} to {system}; Administer {process} per {protocol}
- monitor: Monitor {metric} for {threshold}; Verify {state} remains {condition}; Track {progress} against {target}; Check {status} every {interval}
- modify: Modify {parameter} when {condition}; Update {configuration} to {value}; Adjust {setting} for {optimization}; Maintain {state} within {bounds}
- resolve: Resolve {issue} using {method}; Troubleshoot {problem} via {diagnosis}; Fix {error} within {timeframe}; Handle {exception} with {fallback}
- conclude: Conclude {job} with {summary}; Store {results} in {location}; Finish {process} by {action}; Close {session} after {cleanup}
Avoid:
- Vague, unslotted phrasing such as "Do the thing with the stuff".
- Classifying a statement into a step whose templates it does not match, or using another step's template.
- Filling slots with placeholders or vague nouns just to match the template surface.
Why: Templated phrasings make step classification and acceptance authoring deterministic — the same surface grammar always maps to the same step.
