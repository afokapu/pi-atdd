Title: Define each wagon as one separable, coherent, artifact-bounded unit of a journey
Given: A planner authors or reshapes a wagon in a journey, or composes wagons into a train.
When: The planner names the wagon and sets its boundary, artifacts and features.
Then: Define the wagon as one subject/action/goal unit of the domain, separable from its siblings, that consumes and produces declared artifacts, groups the features decomposing its behavior, and composes into trains without a produce/consume cycle.
Prefer:
- Cut the wagon from siblings along a correct boundary, judged by cross-wagon coupling.
- Align subject, action and goal so the outcome is measurable.
- Consume and produce declared artifacts across contracts.
- Group under the wagon the features that decompose its behavior.
Avoid:
- Wagons forming a produce/consume cycle with siblings in a train.
- Describing how the wagon executes at runtime; that is a coder/tester concern.
- Treating this definition as itself enforced; enforcement lives in the constraint/rule nodes that require it.
Why: A wagon is the planner's domain-decomposition unit; a stable shared meaning lets the enforceable wagon conventions agree on what they protect.
