Title: Keep wagons, features and WMBTs at their canonical plan paths
Given: A wagon, feature or WMBT declaration exists or is being written anywhere in the repository outside vendored/generated trees, with any filename suffix
When: its location is checked or the document is written
Then: Every such declaration must be at its canonical path: wagon at `<plan_root>/<wagon>/_<wagon>.yaml`, feature at `<plan_root>/<wagon>/<feature>.yaml`, WMBT at `<plan_root>/<wagon>/<WMBT>.yaml`.
Prefer:
- On a finding, move the document to the path the finding names.
- Resolve `<plan_root>` from the `topology:` settings in atdd-bun.yaml (default `plan`) rather than hard-coding a directory.
Avoid:
- Keeping a second plan layout alongside the canonical one in the same repository.
- Placing plan documents at ad-hoc paths and relying on identity lookup alone to find them.
Unless:
- Vendored and generated trees (`node_modules`, `dist`, `build`, `_generated`) are never scanned.
Why: The plan is navigated by path as much as by identity. A document at a non-canonical path is found by nothing that walks the layout, and two layouts in one repository make every tool guess.
