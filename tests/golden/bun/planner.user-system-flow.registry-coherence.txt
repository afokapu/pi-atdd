Title: Keep the train registry and per-train documents mutually coherent
Given: At CONFIRM, the corpus contains plan/_trains.yaml and per-train documents under plan/_trains, whatever this plan changed.
When: The planner confirms a plan.
Then: Every registry row must name a path that resolves to an existing file, and every non-underscore per-train document under plan/_trains must be named by a row: no dangling rows, no orphans.
Prefer:
- Plan a train's registry row and document as one change, both derived from its train_id.
- Remove a train's row and document together.
- Fix pre-existing dangling rows or orphans before confirming.
Avoid:
- Writing a row before its document exists (dangling row): consumers resolve through it and some crash.
- Leaving a document no row names (orphan): inert, but residue of document-before-registry ordering.
- Counting underscore sidecars as per-train documents.
- Repointing planner.train.registry's validator ref; the conventions sweep owns it.
Unless:
- Underscore-prefixed sidecars under plan/_trains (_aliases.yaml, _interlockings/) are not per-train documents, so they need no registry row.
Why: Consumers resolve trains through the registry, so a row naming an absent file breaks them; unregistered documents are residue of document-before-registry ordering and must be reportable.
