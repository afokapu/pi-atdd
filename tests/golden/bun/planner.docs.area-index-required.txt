Title: Give each existing canonical docs area an index.adoc
Given: An authored area (a canonical-tree directory holding authored documents: docs/, docs/purpose/, docs/architecture/, docs/architecture/decisions/, docs/archive/) exists or your plan creates it.
When: It lacks index.adoc, before or after your change.
Then: The area MUST contain an index.adoc — its entry point and the node the rendered site navigates to.
Prefer:
- Give the index identity like any document: docs/architecture/index.adoc with `= Architecture`, `:doc-id: architecture.index`, `:status: current`.
- Nothing to introduce? Ask whether the area should exist yet, not what its index says; grow the tree only for a real requirement.
- docs/architecture/decisions/index.adoc is also a DERIVED registry (planner.docs.adr-registry-derived).
Avoid:
- Ceremonial index/canonical files in every subdirectory or to fill an empty taxonomy; dirs with two related docs need no landing page.
- Creating an empty area just to index it.
- Do not require an index in docs/dist/.
Unless:
- docs/dist/ is generated output, never required to carry an index.
- An absent area is not reported; only an existing area missing its index is.
Why: No index means the site can't navigate into the area nor a reader enter it; scoping to canonical areas deliberately avoids ceremonial files.
