Title: Cap acceptances at 5 per WMBT; split the WMBT when more are needed
Given: A WMBT and its acceptances in a plan.
When: The planner authors, revises or reviews the WMBT or any of its acceptances.
Then: Keep at most 5 acceptances per WMBT (acceptance_cap max = 5); if more are needed, split the WMBT into clearer outcomes instead of exceeding the cap.
Prefer:
- Split an over-full WMBT into several WMBTs with clearer outcomes, each with <=5 acceptances.
- Map each acceptance to exactly one test (acceptance_to_test 1:1); several tests may target one component (test_to_component N:1).
- Give each acceptance exactly one metric_id.
- Keep each WMBT to one dimension and one lens_ref; acceptances inherit context from the parent WMBT.
Avoid:
- Exceeding 5 acceptances on a WMBT, or gaming the cap by merging independent checks into one compound acceptance.
- Acceptances with mixed measures (more than one metric_id).
- One acceptance spread across several tests, or several acceptances sharing one test.
- Mixing dimensions or lens_refs within one WMBT.
Why: A WMBT needing more than five acceptances usually hides several outcomes; the cap keeps each WMBT one clear outcome with one measure and one test per acceptance.
