Title: Give every wagon produce/consume entry a version-free contract URN that resolves in the registry
Given: A wagon manifest (plan/{wagon}/*.yaml) has produce/consume entries naming artifacts {domain}:{resource}[.{category}], linked to contracts/_artifacts.yaml.
When: You author/edit those entries, roll URNs out across wagons, or change registry ids or producers.
Then: Each produce/consume entry carries `urn: contract:{domain}:{resource}[.{category}]` (no version) that resolves to a registry id {same}:v{N} whose producer names the producing wagon.
Prefer:
- Derive from the name: domain before the first colon, resource up to an optional dot, category after it; mechanic:decision.choice -> contract:mechanic:decision.choice.
- Same URN on the producer's produce entry and every consume entry (consume names `from wagon:<producer>`).
- Upgrade in order: add urn to all wagon artifacts; validate all resolve; generate missing contracts for orphaned URNs.
Avoid:
- Legacy dot hierarchy contract:mechanic.decision.choice; migrate it.
- Versions (:v1) or schema/API detail in the URN.
- Changing URNs when a contract moves v1->v2.
- Dropping the category: ux:foundations.colors -> contract:ux:foundations.
Unless:
- Pre-URN wagons: urn is optional initially; required for new wagons.
Why: Wagons declare WHAT (URN), contracts HOW (version, schema, API): contracts evolve without wagon YAML changes, names stay readable, and the graph keeps bidirectional wagon<->contract linkage.
