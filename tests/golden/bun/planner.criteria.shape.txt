Title: Give every criteria entry id, description, harness and expected_outcome
Given: One or more criteria entries are present. This covers new, edited and existing unchanged entries, in any artifact.
When: the planner authors, edits or evaluates an artifact that holds those criteria entries
Then: Every criteria entry follows the documented shape: it has all of the required fields id, description, harness and expected_outcome.
Prefer:
- Include all four required fields (id, description, harness, expected_outcome) when you first write the entry.
- When you come across an existing criteria entry that is missing a required field, add the missing field so that it matches the documented shape.
Avoid:
- Criteria entries that leave out any of id, description, harness or expected_outcome.
- Adding extra ad-hoc fields in place of a missing required field.
Why: Criteria are the structured form of an acceptance. Because the shape is fixed, downstream tooling can pick the harness template and resolve the expected outcome without guessing.
