Title: Declare each train's themes explicitly, primary theme first
Given: A train groups themed wagons and may not yet declare a themes list.
When: You create a train, touch one lacking an explicit themes list, change its themes or their order, add/remove/move its wagons, or re-theme a member wagon.
Then: The train declares its themes explicitly as a list whose first entry is its primary theme.
Prefer:
- Most wagons should match the primary (first declared) theme; keep it so when adding, moving or re-theming wagons.
- For a train spanning multiple themes, list the dominant theme first, others after as secondary.
- On a move, recheck both the train left and the train joined.
Avoid:
- Themes only implied by the train's name or wagons.
- Letting most wagons drift to a secondary or undeclared theme.
- Listing many secondary themes just to make off-theme wagons look aligned.
Unless:
- Secondary themes are allowed when a train spans multiple themes; not every wagon needs to match the primary.
Why: The primary theme is the first declared theme, so it exists only if themes are declared explicitly; most wagons matching it keeps the train coherent.
