Title: Keep every referenced train sequence equal to its interlocking route projection
Given: An interlocking route references a train, so the route has a projected sequence and the train has its own sequence.
When: Any referenced train sequence is checked at CONFIRM — whether or not you edited the route or the train in this plan (editing either side is the common way to introduce a mismatch).
Then: Make the referenced train sequence equal the interlocking route projection step by step on step, intent, from, to and artifact.
Prefer:
- Change the route and the referenced train sequence in the same plan step so they stay equal.
- Compare step by step on all five fields (step, intent, from, to, artifact) for every referenced train before handing off to CONFIRM, including untouched ones.
Avoid:
- Matching only some fields (e.g. same steps but different artifact or from/to) and calling the sequences equivalent.
- Editing only one side and leaving the other to drift.
- Removing the train reference or editing the validator's expected projection only to get rid of the failure.
Why: Guard coverage and the rest of the route reasoning are proven against the route-to-train projection, so a train that diverges from its route breaks that proof. The check is strict and blocks CONFIRM.
