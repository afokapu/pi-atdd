Title: no-consume-cycle is the exact invariant; separability and granularity signals stay advisory until calibrated
Given: A wagon-coupling finding is being handled, or wagon-coupling enforcement is being designed, implemented or promoted.
When: Deciding how binding a wagon-coupling check is.
Then: planner.wagon.no-consume-cycle is the first enforcement and the exact invariant. Separability and granularity signals are advisory. Calibrate them before any promotion to strict. Promotion after calibration remains possible.
Prefer:
- Conservative graph proxies: cross-wagon fan-in/fan-out, shared-artifact adjacency, SCCs, community-cut signals.
Avoid:
- Promoting separability or granularity signals to strict without calibrating them first.
- Requiring Henry-Kafura (fan_in x fan_out)^2 or treewidth computations as day-one enforcement. They are rationale only.
Why: Henry-Kafura and treewidth back the super-linear model conceptually. Enforcement uses conservative proxies so that validators do not become noisy before corpus calibration.
