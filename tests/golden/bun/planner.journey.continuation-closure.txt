Title: Close every journey's continuation topology
Given: A plan declares one or more journeys: each an entrypoint interlocking plus the interlockings reachable through continuations.
When: The plan reaches CONFIRM (always, for every declared journey, changed or not); also earlier whenever you author or change routes, continuations, terminals, entrypoints, or the trains those routes select.
Then: Account for every reachable interlocking route exactly once: one continuation or one terminal, never both, never none.
Prefer:
- Walk from the entrypoint, listing each (interlocking_id, route_id) with its one outgoing declaration.
- Declare an explicit terminal for each route that ends the journey.
- At CONFIRM, re-run the closure walk over every declared journey, not only the ones you edited.
Avoid:
- Reusing a route_id inside one reachable interlocking; it must be unique.
- Any continuation whose source route does not exist, whose artifact does not occur in the linear train that route selects, or whose destination interlocking does not exist; all three are mandatory.
- Continuations or terminals on interlockings unreachable from the entrypoint; they are invalid.
- Continuation cycles, even intended loops, until an explicit loop construct defines bounded/intentional repetition.
- Implicit code-only handoffs or duplicate continuations.
Why: Interlocking bilateral binding closes declaration→runtime→trace for one routing decision only; journey closure makes handoffs explicit, preventing silent gaps, duplicate continuations, disconnected fragments and artifact-free handoffs.
