Title: Register a subject before typing any train under it
Given: A plan contains a typed train train:<subject>:<slug> or a subject entry in plan/_subjects.yaml
When: the planner types or re-identifies a train, edits the subject registry, or the plan reaches CONFIRM
Then: Ensure the <subject> exists in plan/_subjects.yaml before any train:<subject>:<slug> is typed under it.
Prefer:
- Add the subject entry to plan/_subjects.yaml first, then type trains under it
- Choose as subject the durable object the train changes, written as a kebab-case noun-led name (this naming is mandatory, see avoid[1])
- Reuse an existing registered subject when the train changes the same durable object
Avoid:
- Typing train:<subject>:<slug> under an unregistered subject
- Any subject (registry entry or typed train) that is not ALL of: kebab-case, noun-led, and the durable object of the train's change. This is mandatory, not a preference; camelCase/snake_case/other non-kebab names fail, and a verb, an actor, a theme, a route, a category, an owner or a program name is never a valid subject
- Duplicate subject entries in the registry (registry entries are unique-by-subject)
- Two typed trains with the same subject+slug (typed trains are unique-by-subject+slug)
- Leaving an existing misnamed or duplicated subject/identity in place because the current edit did not change it; CONFIRM validates the whole registry
Why: A train's identity is its subject plus slug, so the subject must be a registered, durable noun and identities must be unique for every typed train to resolve to exactly one stable subject.
