Title: Adapt only among declared valid strategies
Given: Work involves selecting among two or more already declared valid strategies based on workload or resource signals, whether those signals are present or missing
When: planning, specifying, implementing or reviewing that selection
Then: Choose only among the declared strategies via a deterministic, inspectable selection policy with a stable fallback, preserving accepted outputs and route obligations.
Prefer:
- Use adaptive selection only where measured workload variation justifies multiple strategies and switching overhead.
- Acceptances testing thresholds, missing metrics, fallback, oscillation and equivalence across workload classes; benchmark total cost.
- Interlocking guards selecting between deterministic alternatives.
Avoid:
- Inventing routes or strategies.
- Suppressing failure behavior when signals are absent.
- Inferring unimplemented orchestration (e.g. dynamic rescheduling) from this pattern; it needs new validated capabilities.
- Treating this as enforcement or a new runtime dispatch option; it is advisory guidance and existing plan and source validators still apply.
Why: Multiple strategies are only worth their switching overhead where measured workload variation justifies them, and selection must keep accepted outputs and route obligations intact.
