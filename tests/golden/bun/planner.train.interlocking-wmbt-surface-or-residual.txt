Title: Surface every WMBT obligation in the interlocking or declare a structural residual
Given: A train interlocking is the subject of CONFIRM validation and projects WMBT obligations.
When: CONFIRM checks that the interlocking covers its WMBT obligations.
Then: Every WMBT obligation must surface in the interlocking as a message, self-message, guard, fragment, or invariant, or as an explicit structural residual.
Prefer:
- For each WMBT ref, either show it with one of the five surface kinds or list it as an explicit structural residual with a residual id that names the WMBT ref.
- Use the notation the project's interlocking validator already recognises; copy the form of existing passing interlockings.
Avoid:
- Leaving a WMBT ref with neither a surface nor an explicit structural residual (silent omission).
- Adding vague catch-all diagram elements that satisfy coverage without expressing the obligation.
Why: The interlocking is a projection that must cover its source WMBT obligations; an obligation that neither surfaces nor is declared residual is silently lost from the train's behavioural contract.
