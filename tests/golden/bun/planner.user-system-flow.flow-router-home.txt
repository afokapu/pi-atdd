Title: Place every interlocking artifact under plan/_trains/_interlockings/
Given: A plan contains, or is gaining, interlocking artifacts (new or existing); placement is enforced at CONFIRM, so it applies from the moment the interlocking is authored
When: The planner authors, edits or moves an interlocking artifact, or brings a plan containing one to CONFIRM
Then: Every interlocking artifact lives under plan/_trains/_interlockings/.
Prefer:
- Place interlocking files under plan/_trains/_interlockings/ when first authoring them, rather than relocating them at CONFIRM
- Ensure each interlocking under the home has an entry in the registry index plan/_trains/_interlockings.yaml (separate registration obligation, see splitSuggestion)
Avoid:
- Placing interlocking artifacts next to wagons, trains or in other plan folders outside plan/_trains/_interlockings/
- Leaving an interlocking artifact unregistered in plan/_trains/_interlockings.yaml
Why: Other interlocking rules (registry mirroring, guards, payloads, route resolution, route-space admission) presuppose the homed and registered interlocking set; an artifact outside the home or registry is invisible to them.
