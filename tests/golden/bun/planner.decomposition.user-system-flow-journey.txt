Title: Decode the nominal train and its keep/pivot/kill sibling trains
Given: Wagons and WMBTs for a main job have been kept.
When: The planner decodes the journey that realizes that main job.
Then: Define the nominal train (the happy-path journey realizing the main job) and, alongside it, decide each error, alternate and exception sibling train with an explicit operator keep, pivot or kill.
Prefer:
- Decode the nominal train first, then enumerate error, alternate and exception siblings against it.
- Record the keep/pivot/kill decision for every sibling train next to the nominal train.
Avoid:
- Decoding trains before wagons and WMBTs have been kept.
- Planning only the happy path and leaving error/alternate/exception journeys undecided.
- Adding sibling trains without an operator keep/pivot/kill decision.
Why: The nominal and sibling trains are what a journey composes across interlockings; decoding them after wagons/WMBTs are kept, with explicit keep/pivot/kill on siblings, makes non-happy-path scope a deliberate decision rather than an omission.
