Title: Keep every interlocking registry entry in sync with its document
Given: The interlocking registry (plan/_trains/_interlockings.yaml) indexes interlocking documents plan/_trains/_interlockings/<id>.yaml by path
When: the plan reaches CONFIRM, for every indexed entry, regardless of which files or fields the current change touched
Then: every registry entry's theme, status and surfaces must equal the values in the document it points at.
Prefer:
- Treat the document as authoritative: edit the document first, then copy theme, status and surfaces into the registry entry in the same change.
- When a mismatch is found — including a pre-existing one your change did not cause — fix the registry entry to match the document, not the other way round.
Avoid:
- Changing the document to match a stale registry entry — on disagreement the registry is always the defect.
- Updating a document's status, theme or surfaces without updating its registry entry (or vice versa).
- Treating an existing mismatch as out of scope because your change did not touch the registry or that document.
- Adding fields to the registry that diverge from or are not sourced from the document.
Why: The document is authoritative and the registry is only discovery metadata that denormalises theme, status and surfaces from it; any disagreement means the registry is lying to whoever discovers interlockings through it.
