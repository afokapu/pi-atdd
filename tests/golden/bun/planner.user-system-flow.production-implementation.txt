Title: Plan trains as production orchestration run by TrainRunner
Given: A train is involved in any planner artifact (plan, wagon, WMBT, acceptance or doc)
When: the planner specifies, references or describes that train
Then: Treat the train as production orchestration (not test infrastructure), located under python/trains/ and executed by TrainRunner (python/trains/runner.py).
Prefer:
- Name TrainRunner, the production executor of a train, as the executor.
- Reference atdd/coder/conventions/train.convention.yaml as where the train implementation patterns are documented.
Avoid:
- Planning trains as test infrastructure (fixtures, harnesses, test-only helpers) or placing them under test directories.
- Stating an executor or location for trains other than TrainRunner under python/trains/.
Why: Trains are production orchestration executed by TrainRunner under python/trains/; classifying them as test infrastructure misstates what they are and where they live.
