Title: Decompose every job into the nine ordered JTBD steps
Given: A job-to-be-done is being framed or decomposed, in any artifact or phase.
When: The planner decomposes the job into steps or names/orders its steps.
Then: Decompose every job into the ordered sequence of nine JTBD steps — define, locate, prepare, confirm, execute, monitor, modify, resolve, conclude — each with its fixed purpose, guiding question and expected outputs.
Prefer:
- 1 define — purpose: frame the work and set boundaries; question: What must be defined upfront before anything else can proceed?; outputs: clear objectives, success metrics, constraint boundaries.
- 2 locate — purpose: identify and access required inputs or resources; question: What must be found or accessed to do the job?; outputs: resource inventory, access patterns, dependency map.
- 3 prepare — purpose: make the environment/resources ready without starting execution; question: How must the executor prepare before acting?; outputs: prepared resources, validated inputs, ready state confirmation.
- 4 confirm — purpose: ensure preconditions and synchronization before moving forward; question: What must be verified or agreed upon to continue?; outputs: precondition checklist, permission verification, go/no-go decision.
- 5 execute — purpose: carry out the core action that changes state; question: What must the executor do to perform the job itself?; outputs: completed core job, state changes, performance metrics.
- 6 monitor — purpose: observe progress, quality, or risks to enable correction; question: What must be tracked during execution to ensure success?; outputs: progress indicators, performance data, anomaly reports.
- 7 modify — purpose: proactively adjust or optimize during normal operation; question: What might need to be changed for better outcomes?; outputs: adjustments made, optimization results, updated configurations.
- 8 resolve — purpose: reactive problem-solving and exception handling; question: What problems must be addressed when things go wrong?; outputs: problem resolutions, recovery procedures, workaround documentation.
- 9 conclude — purpose: close the loop and capture results; question: What must be done to finish the job and capture results?; outputs: completion confirmation, results captured, final documentation.
- Use each step's leading and supporting key terms (lists in verifier_detail) as signals for classifying a job step from its phrasing.
Avoid:
- Inventing step names outside the nine, so the job can no longer be located in the lifecycle.
- Mixing modify (proactive) with resolve (reactive), or starting execution inside prepare.
- Ordering steps against the canonical 1–9 sequence (e.g. execute before confirm).
Why: The nine steps give a stable, exhaustive vocabulary for framing work so that any job-to-be-done can be located in the lifecycle and decomposed consistently across planners.
