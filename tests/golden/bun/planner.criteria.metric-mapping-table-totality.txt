Title: Keep the dimension->metric table total over the schema's enums
Given: The dimension->metric table (term dimension_metric) or wmbt.schema.json's dimension/direction enums.
When: You edit either.
Then: The table keeps exactly one row per dimension x direction pair (24 today), at the schema's spelling, each naming a metric_id or `unmapped` with its reason under unmapped_pairs.
Prefer:
- Give both spellings of one intent (minimize/decrease, maximize/increase) the same metric_id.
- Key rows at the schema's exact spelling — the schema owns both vocabularies.
- Write each unmapped reason so a reader can tell a deliberate omission from a missing row.
Avoid:
- A missing or misspelled row that lets a pair fall through silently.
- Mapping one spelling of an intent but not its synonym.
- Reusing a downward metric as an upward default — mark the pair `unmapped` instead.
Why: Nothing may fall through the table silently; 102 of 472 WMBTs once mapped to nothing because the table spelled `decrease` where they wrote `minimize`.
