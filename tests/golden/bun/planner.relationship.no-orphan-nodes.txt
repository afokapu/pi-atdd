Title: Reference every convention node in at least one relationship edge
Given: The repository has convention nodes (*.convention.yaml with a rule_id) and a relationship graph; this holds for all nodes, pre-existing or newly planned.
When: Relationship-graph coverage is evaluated or you plan any change touching convention nodes or graph edges.
Then: Ensure every non-excluded convention node's rule_id appears as source_ref or target_ref of at least one relationship-graph edge; a node referenced by no edge is an orphan and is refused.
Prefer:
- For each orphan (new or pre-existing), author at least one edge (via `atdd author relationship`) with it as source_ref or target_ref, using a real relationship type/foundation to a semantically related node.
- Plan the edge in the same change that introduces or renames a node.
Avoid:
- Treating 'no dangling edges' as enough — the graph must cover every node.
- Placeholder edges to unrelated nodes or with arbitrary type/foundation to silence the check.
- Moving a real node under tests/fixtures or into demo.* to escape the check.
Unless:
- The node is under a path containing tests/fixtures or in the demo.* namespace — excluded.
Why: Reverse half of graph integrity: the forward check forbids edges to missing nodes, this forbids nodes no edge reaches — no dangling edges AND no orphan nodes.
