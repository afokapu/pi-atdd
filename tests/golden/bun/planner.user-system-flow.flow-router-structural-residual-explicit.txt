Title: Make every interlocking structural residual explicit, narrow, and bound to an acceptance and validator
Given: An interlocking subject (standalone or embedded) that has one or more structural residuals, whether declared or relied on without being written down.
When: The planner authors or edits the interlocking, or the interlocking is validated at CONFIRM.
Then: Make each structural residual explicit and narrow, and bind it to both an acceptance reference and a validator reference.
Prefer:
- Write each residual down with its own residual id, stating the one structural gap it covers.
- Bind each residual to a concrete acceptance_ref and validator_ref that resolve to an existing acceptance and validator.
Avoid:
- Leaving a structural residual implicit: relying on it without writing it down as a residual.
- Broad residuals that cover several unrelated gaps.
- Missing, placeholder, or dangling acceptance_ref or validator_ref added just to satisfy the presence check.
Why: A structural residual is the escape hatch from surfacing something directly; it stays safe only if it is explicit, narrow, and still covered by an acceptance and a validator.
