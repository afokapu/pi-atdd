Title: Create train variations within a theme by mode, complexity, persona, or phase
Given: A planner is defining or revising train variations for a theme (new or existing).
When: Creating or revising those train variations.
Then: Create the train variations within the theme by mode, complexity, persona, or phase.
Prefer:
- By mode, e.g. train:match:solo / train:match:duel / train:match:team.
- By complexity, e.g. train:decision:core / train:decision:timeout / train:decision:preview.
- By persona, e.g. train:player:registration / train:partnership:sponsor-registration.
- By phase, e.g. train:league:tournament-setup / train:league:tournament-active / train:league:tournament-complete.
Why: The source states no rationale; the four strategies are the canonical ways to vary trains within a theme.
