Title: Keep train and wagon references bidirectional (Train - Wagon Coverage)
Given: A plan containing trains and/or wagon manifests (either side may be empty), plus the wagons_not_in_train allow-list
When: the planner adds, removes, renames, or changes the status of a wagon; adds, removes, or edits a train's participants; or changes the wagons_not_in_train allow-list
Then: Keep train-wagon coverage bidirectional with no orphan: every wagon appears in at least one train's participants (wagon -> train), and every `wagon:` participant of a train has a wagon manifest (train -> wagon).
Prefer:
- When creating a wagon, add it as a participant of at least one train in the same change.
- When adding a `wagon:` participant to a train, create or point to an existing wagon manifest in the same change.
- When renaming or removing a wagon, update every train participant that references it.
- When removing a wagon from wagons_not_in_train or promoting it from draft, add it to a train in the same change.
Avoid:
- Leaving an orphan on either side: a wagon in no train, or a train participant with no wagon manifest.
- Adding a wagon to the wagons_not_in_train allow-list or marking it status:draft just to silence the coverage check.
Unless:
- wagon -> train direction only: the wagon is listed in the wagons_not_in_train allow-list or has status:draft.
Why: Bidirectional coverage means each side references the other with no orphan: every non-exempt wagon is reachable from a train, and every `wagon:` participant of a train resolves to a wagon manifest.
