Title: Bind each Bun test's Acceptance/WMBT header to a URN the plan declares
Given: The repository's plan declares acceptances and a Bun test carries an `// Acceptance:` or `// WMBT:` binding header
When: the binding is checked (test or header changed, plan changed, or any CI run)
Then: Every `// Acceptance:` and `// WMBT:` URN in the header MUST be stated somewhere under plan/**.
Prefer:
- Repair the binding from the plan, not the plan from the test: the plan is authoritative during test authoring.
- Stale header or renamed acceptance: point the test at the declared acceptance it actually exercises and proves (illustrative: `// Acceptance: acc:<wagon>:<ID>` where that URN is stated in a plan/<wagon>/ document; `// WMBT: wmbt:<wagon>:<WMBT>`).
- Test proves different behavior: bind it to that behavior's existing declared acceptance; if it does not prove the claimed acceptance, adjust the test, not the label.
- No declared acceptance fits: return to the authorized planning flow so the behavior is declared deliberately before the test claims coverage.
Avoid:
- Creating, renaming or repurposing a plan acceptance, or inventing one, merely to make a test resolve or clear this finding; plan additions are planner work justified by the behavior.
- Binding to a convenient but semantically unrelated declared acceptance (the dangerous gaming case).
Unless:
- The plan declares no acceptances at all: NOT_APPLICABLE.
- The test has no binding header: owned by tester.bun.acceptance-binding-declared (no double-count).
Why: A binding to an acceptance that exists nowhere attributes the test to nothing, making coverage figures fiction: a NAME accepted where a CLAIM is required. Strict (severity 4): a finding is not suppressible and fails CI (see shared:policy.strict-disposition).
