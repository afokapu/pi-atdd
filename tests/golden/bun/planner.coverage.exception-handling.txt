Title: Declare coverage exceptions only through config allow-lists or draft status
Given: A plan artifact (wagon, feature or WMBT) intentionally does not satisfy full coverage
When: the planner records that coverage gap
Then: Declare the exception only through the matching coverage.exceptions.* config allow-list entry or by marking the artifact status: draft — never leave the gap undeclared.
Prefer:
- List wagons that are utility, shared or under development in coverage.exceptions.wagons_not_in_train.
- List deprecated or experimental features in coverage.exceptions.features_orphaned.
- List draft/placeholder WMBTs in coverage.exceptions.wmbts_without_acceptance.
- Mark genuinely unfinished artifacts status: draft; draft status exempts them from full coverage (COVERAGE-PLAN-2.1 and COVERAGE-PLAN-2.4) only.
Avoid:
- Leaving a coverage gap with no allow-list entry or draft status.
- Allow-listing an artifact in a list whose use case does not fit it (e.g. putting a finished, live wagon in wagons_not_in_train).
- Marking finished artifacts draft just to silence coverage checks, or assuming draft exempts from checks other than COVERAGE-PLAN-2.1 and 2.4.
Why: Coverage checks are strict; explicit, config-recorded exceptions keep intentional gaps visible and reviewable instead of hiding real missing coverage.
