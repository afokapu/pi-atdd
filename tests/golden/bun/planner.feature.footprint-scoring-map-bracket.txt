Title: Map a feature's footprint points to its size bracket
Given: A feature's footprint points have been computed, with the ceiling applied.
When: The planner assigns or revises the feature's size bracket.
Then: Map the points to a bracket: XS ≤5, S 6–10, M 11–15, L 16–25, XL ≥26.
Prefer:
- Map the ceiled integer points, not the raw fractional sum.
Avoid:
- Choosing a bracket by feel, or copying the worked examples' 'S' labels where they disagree with the table.
Why: A fixed points-to-bracket table makes sizes comparable across features.
