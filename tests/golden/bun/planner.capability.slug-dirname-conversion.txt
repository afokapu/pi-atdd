Title: Convert slugs and filesystem names by swapping hyphens and underscores
Given: A planner artifact needs a wagon or feature slug or its filesystem counterpart (directory name or feature .yaml filename).
When: Deriving a slug from a name, or converting between a slug and its directory/filename in either direction.
Then: Use only the conversion functions: derive slug = lowercase the name, replace spaces/underscores with hyphens; slug→dirname = slug.replace('-', '_'); dirname→slug = dirname.replace('_', '-'); feature slug→filename = feature_slug.replace('-', '_') + '.yaml'; filename→slug = filename.replace('_', '-').replace('.yaml', '').
Prefer:
- Verb-object phrasing for slugs (preferred, not required).
Avoid:
- Any other slug↔filesystem transformation (abbreviating, reordering, dropping words).
- Hyphens in wagon directory or feature filenames; spaces, underscores or capitals in slugs.
- Omitting or doubling the .yaml suffix.
Why: Logical slugs are kebab-case and filesystem names snake_case; the conversion functions are pure hyphen/underscore swaps between them.
