Title: Order and number the steps of a wagon procedure
Given: A document contains a wagon procedure written as steps
When: the step list is written, or any step is added, removed, moved, renumbered or edited
Then: Steps are in an explicit order and each step carries a number.
Prefer:
- After any edit, including numbering-only edits, re-check that every step still has a number and the order is unambiguous.
Avoid:
- Unnumbered or unordered steps whose execution order a reader must guess.
Why: Ordered, numbered steps with pre-conditions and outputs make a wagon procedure reproducible and checkable.
