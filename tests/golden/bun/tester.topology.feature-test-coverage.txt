Title: Bind at least one test to an acceptance of every feature that owns WMBTs
Given: A plan feature owns one or more WMBTs (behaviours declared for it in the plan)
When: the topology gate checks whether any test is bound to that feature's acceptances
Then: At least one test is bound to one of the feature's acceptances.
Prefer:
- Add a test beneath the feature bound to one of its acceptances.
Avoid:
- Leaving declared feature behaviour (WMBTs) with no test bound to any of its acceptances.
Unless:
- Vendored and generated trees (`node_modules`, `dist`, `build`, `_generated`) are never scanned.
Why: Behaviour declared and never tested is a silent gap.
