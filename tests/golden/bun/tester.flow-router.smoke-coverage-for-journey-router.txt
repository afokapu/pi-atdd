Title: Smoke-test every exposed Station Master action through the real entrypoint
Given: An interlocking declares `entrypoint.exposed: true`, which requires at least one Station Master action (the user-facing route surface)
When: Any such exposed action exists (e.g. added, edited, or flagged by the coverage detector)
Then: Each exposed action MUST have an `e2e/**/*.ts` smoke test driving it through the real entrypoint -> Station Master -> InterlockingRunner -> TrainRunner path.
Prefer:
- Call the real action endpoint; assert the Station Master chose the interlocking (InterlockingRunner) path, not a direct train.
- Assert InterlockingRunner resolved a route, TrainRunner ran the selected train, and the trace records interlockingId/routeId/selectedTrainId.
- If the interlocking is genuinely internal, set `entrypoint.exposed: false` with a reason; it leaves scope.
Avoid:
- Bypassing the real entrypoint/Station Master or invoking a train directly.
- Merely naming the action, Station Master and runners without asserting route, train execution and trace ids.
- Flipping a user-facing action to `exposed: false` to escape coverage.
Unless:
- `entrypoint.exposed: false` (internal-transition-only / library-only / planned-not-exposed): no Station Master action, out of scope.
Why: Exposed actions are critical user-facing routes; only an end-to-end smoke test proves the interlocking path and production runners execute. Criticality comes mechanically from `entrypoint.exposed`.
