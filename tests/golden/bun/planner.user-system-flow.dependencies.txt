Title: Reference train prerequisites as train:{train_id}
Given: A train spec exists or is being authored, edited, reviewed or validated, with or without existing dependency entries
When: The planner writes, changes or checks that train's dependencies
Then: Every entry in the train's dependencies must be a prerequisite train reference of the form train:{train_id} matching ^train:[0-9]{2}-[a-z][a-z0-9-]*$.
Prefer:
- Foundation (commons) trains, such as authentication/session foundations, often precede the trains that build on them (foundation_first); order them first when that applies — this is a frequent pattern, not an unconditional requirement.
Avoid:
- A data-flow consumer train that does not depend on the train producing the data it consumes — consumers must depend on producers (data_flow).
- Circular dependencies between trains — direct, transitive, or a train listing itself — are forbidden.
- Free-text, bare-id or wagon references in the dependencies list instead of train:{train_id}.
- Reversing direction: making a producer depend on its consumer.
Why: A dependency is a prerequisite train reference; a uniform train:{train_id} form lets tooling resolve ordering, consumers need their producers first, and a cycle makes the trains impossible to sequence.
