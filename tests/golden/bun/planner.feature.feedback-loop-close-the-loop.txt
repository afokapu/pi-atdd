Title: Feedback-loop must close the loop
Given: A feature is marked kind: feedback-loop.
When: You author or revise its plan, WMBTs and acceptances.
Then: Declare at least one SMOKE acceptance with a close_the_loop block containing both consumer_reacted and drift_resolved.
Prefer:
- Follow the source example shape, e.g. acc:<wagon>:<wmbt_id>-SMOKE-NNN-close-the-loop with close_the_loop:{consumer_reacted, drift_resolved}.
- Define success as all three: (a) producer detected a condition and emitted a correction, (b) consumer received and reacted (consumer_reacted), and (c) the producer's predicate no longer fires next cycle (drift_resolved).
Avoid:
- Acceptances that stop at 'producer emitted a correction' without proving the consumer reacted.
- Proving the consumer reacted without proving the drift is gone on the next cycle.
- A close_the_loop block missing either required field, or placing it on a non-SMOKE acceptance.
Why: A feedback loop only works if the correction is consumed and the triggering condition disappears; emission alone proves nothing about the loop closing.
