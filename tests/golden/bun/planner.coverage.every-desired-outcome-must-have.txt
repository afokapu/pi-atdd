Title: Give every non-draft WMBT at least one acceptance
Given: A plan contains a WMBT whose status is not draft and that is not on the wmbts_without_acceptance allow-list
When: WMBT acceptance coverage is checked or reviewed (any existing or new WMBT, regardless of what the current change touches)
Then: The WMBT must have at least one acceptance criterion linked to it.
Prefer:
- Write at least one acceptance for each WMBT in the same change that adds the WMBT or moves it out of draft.
- Keep a WMBT in status:draft until at least one acceptance exists for it.
Avoid:
- Moving a WMBT out of draft before any acceptance exists for it.
- Removing the last acceptance of a non-draft WMBT.
- Attaching a placeholder or empty acceptance just to satisfy the count (gaming the metric).
- Putting a WMBT on the wmbts_without_acceptance allow-list, or setting it back to draft, just to silence the check.
Unless:
- The WMBT has status:draft.
- The WMBT is listed on the wmbts_without_acceptance allow-list.
Why: Acceptance coverage (wmbt_acceptance_coverage) requires every non-draft WMBT to carry at least one acceptance; a WMBT without one has no acceptance to verify it against.
