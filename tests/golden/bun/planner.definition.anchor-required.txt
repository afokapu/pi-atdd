Title: Keep a connected family-kind definition anchor for every core domain artifact
Given: Core domain artifacts: theme, train, interlocking, wagon, feature, wmbt, artifact.
When: You change planner convention nodes or relationship edges, or the anchor validator reports a finding.
Then: Each core artifact must have a `planner.<artifact>.definition` node of kind family that is a source_ref or target_ref of at least one relationship edge; a missing, non-family or orphan anchor is refused.
Prefer:
- Author a missing anchor as src/atdd/planner/conventions/nodes/planner.<artifact>.definition.convention.yaml with kind family, then wire at least one edge from it via `atdd author relationship`.
Avoid:
- Making an anchor kind rule/constraint or binding a validator to it; it is transitively protected by the constraint/rule nodes it requires.
- Creating the anchor but leaving it an orphan.
Unless:
- Only the seven core domain artifacts must carry an anchor; other conventions are unaffected.
Why: The anchor gives each core artifact a stable meaning without binding a validator; it must both exist (this rule) and be graph-connected (planner.relationship.no-orphan-nodes).
