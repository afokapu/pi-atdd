Title: Keep every wagon's _features.yaml listing every feature module with its current status
Given: Any wagon and its _features.yaml (the features manifest)
When: The planner authors, updates or relies on that wagon's plan artifacts, or finds the manifest out of step with the wagon's feature modules
Then: The wagon's _features.yaml lists every feature module of the wagon together with its current status.
Prefer:
- Update _features.yaml in the same change that adds, removes, renames or re-statuses a feature module.
- Treat _features.yaml as the wagon's single index of feature modules and statuses.
- Correct an existing omission or stale status when you encounter it, even if your triggering edit was elsewhere.
Avoid:
- Feature modules that exist under the wagon but are missing from _features.yaml.
- Entries whose status is stale or no longer matches the feature's actual state.
- Entries for feature modules that no longer exist.
Why: The features manifest is the wagon's index of its feature modules and statuses; feature registration and wagon-feature coverage checks read it, so an incomplete or stale manifest breaks them.
