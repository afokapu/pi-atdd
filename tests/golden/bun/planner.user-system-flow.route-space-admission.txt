Title: Give every train exactly one route or a typed single-route assessment
Given: The plan's registered trains and the routes of all registered interlockings.
When: The plan reaches CONFIRM (every registered train is checked, whatever changed).
Then: Make each registered train exactly one of: route-targeted (selected by exactly one route across all registered interlockings) or typed single-route (route_space.classification single-route with a closed-vocabulary basis) — never both, never neither.
Prefer:
- Route-targeted: exactly one route across all registered interlockings selects the train.
- Typed single-route: classification single-route plus a basis from the closed vocabulary.
Avoid:
- No route and no classification, assuming that implies direct dispatch — an omission (issue #1554).
- Declaring single-route on a route-targeted train — a contradiction.
- Free prose as the single-route basis.
- Treating a train selected by zero or several routes as route-targeted; only a count of exactly one qualifies.
Why: Route-space was optional metadata, so a train with no route analysis looked like one whose analysis concluded single-route; silence read as proof analysis happened. Explicit classification surfaces the omission at planning time.
