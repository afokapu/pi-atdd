Title: Model each exchanged artifact as a theme-namespaced, URN-identified, location-mirroring unit
Given: A plan, wagon, WMBT, acceptance or planning doc declares or references a unit wagons produce or consume (material, contract, schema).
When: The planner names or decomposes that exchanged unit.
Then: Model it as an artifact: a domain unit naming WHAT is exchanged, URN-identified, theme-namespaced, following theme:hierarchy:aspect.variant, with identity mirroring its declared location.
Prefer:
- Give it a structured URN (artifact-naming.urn-structure, .schema-identifier).
- Make the top segment a canonical theme (.theme-taxonomy).
- Colon for hierarchy, dot for variant (.naming-pattern, .separator-semantics).
- Mirror the physical/contract location (.contract-file-mapping, .logical-physical-mapping).
- Reference it from the WMBTs and acceptances that prove it.
Avoid:
- Describing it as runtime cargo in flight — a coder/tester concern.
- Citing this anchor as the enforcement point; the requiring rule nodes enforce it.
Why: A stable shared meaning of 'artifact' lets wagons, WMBTs and acceptances agree on WHAT is exchanged; enforceable parts live in the naming rules that require this anchor.
