Title: Name the WMBT object of control as a kebab-case noun phrase
Given: A planner is authoring or revising a WMBT
When: the WMBT's object of control (the controlled subject of the WMBT) is set or changed
Then: Synthesize the specific thing being controlled or manipulated as a kebab-case noun phrase.
Prefer:
- A specific noun phrase in kebab-case naming the controlled subject, e.g. `user-profile`, `payment-flow`.
Avoid:
- Verbs, actions or full sentences as the object of control instead of a noun phrase.
- Non-kebab-case forms (spaces, camelCase, snake_case, uppercase).
Why: A WMBT is scoped to one object of control, and the WMBT statement template consumes it, so it must be a specific, consistently formatted noun phrase.
