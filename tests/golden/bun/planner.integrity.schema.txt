Title: Keep every plan artifact valid against its package-shipped schema
Given: A recognized plan artifact (wagon, feature, WMBT and its acceptances, train, train interlocking, journey topology, registry) outside vendored/generated trees — new, edited or already present
When: The planner authors or edits plan artifacts, or plan validation runs over the repository
Then: Every such artifact satisfies its JSON Schema in `planner-schemas/`.
Prefer:
- Correct the field the finding names, as the schema requires.
- Check the artifact's schema in `planner-schemas/` before adding or renaming fields.
Avoid:
- Leaving a schema violation in place on the assumption that downstream rules will tolerate it — they assume the schema shape and break silently.
- Editing or loosening the package-shipped schema to make an artifact pass.
Unless:
- Vendored and generated trees (`node_modules`, `dist`, `build`, `_generated`) are never scanned.
Why: The schemas are the shape every other rule assumes. An artifact that breaks its schema breaks those assumptions silently.
