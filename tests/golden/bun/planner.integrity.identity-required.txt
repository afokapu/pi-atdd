Title: Give every plan artifact its canonical identity
Given: Any plan artifact whose kind the planner recognizes, outside vendored or generated trees, whether new, edited, or existing and unchanged
When: the plan set is authored or validated
Then: The artifact carries its canonical identity for its kind: `train_id`, `interlocking_id`, `journey_id`, or `urn`.
Prefer:
- Add the identity field for the artifact's kind, e.g. `interlocking_id: interlocking:checkout`; when you find an existing artifact without one, add it.
- Use the same identity in every other plan artifact that references this one.
Avoid:
- Adding a placeholder identity just to pass the check; the identity must be the canonical one others reference.
- Using another kind's identity field (e.g. `urn` on an interlocking that needs `interlocking_id`).
Unless:
- Vendored and generated trees (`node_modules`, `dist`, `build`, `_generated`) are never scanned.
Why: An artifact without an identity cannot be referenced, bound, or traced, and every rule that resolves references silently skips it, so its gaps go unseen.
