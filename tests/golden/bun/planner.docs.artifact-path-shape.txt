Title: Declare documentation artifacts as .adoc paths under docs/, with archives under docs/archive/
Given: Your plan's `documentation:` declaration (recorded at RATIFY) is `impact: change` with `artifacts:` (each an `action` create/modify/archive, a `path`, and for archive a `from`).
When: You write each artifact's `path`.
Then: Every artifact `path` begins `docs/` and ends `.adoc`, and every `action: archive` artifact's `path` lies beneath `docs/archive/`.
Prefer:
- Shape the declaration to the tree: create/modify paths beginning `docs/` and ending `.adoc`; archive as `from: <legacy doc>` plus `path: docs/archive/<name>.adoc`.
- An archived document keeps its content and gains `:status:`.
- If no docs change, declare `impact: none` with a reason; never omit the block.
Avoid:
- Archiving outside `docs/archive/` (e.g. into `purpose/` or `architecture/`, which hold only accepted-truth-today documents).
- Declaring markdown or non-`docs/` paths and leaving the mismatch to surface at COMPLETE.
- Treating an absent declaration as `impact: none` — absence blocks as COULD_NOT_CHECK.
Unless:
- `impact: none` declares no artifacts and is not checked by this rule.
- An archive artifact's `from` need not end `.adoc`: it is the legacy markdown being migrated.
Why: The declaration is a promise about paths; checking its shape at RATIFY catches it before the work, not at COMPLETE. The archive half is load-bearing: archiving outside `docs/archive/` quietly promotes history into current truth, the likeliest failure under time pressure.
