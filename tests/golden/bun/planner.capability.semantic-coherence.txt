Title: Keep each wagon definition semantically coherent
Given: Any wagon definition (complete or incomplete)
When: the planner creates, edits or validates that wagon definition
Then: Make the wagon semantically coherent: the subject can perform the action, the action serves the goal, the outcome is a measurable result of the action, and the context makes sense for when/where the action occurs.
Prefer:
- Phrase the outcome as a measurable result of the action
- Declare as produce only artifacts this wagon owns, and mark at least one produce artifact scope: 'external'
- Declare as consume only artifacts owned by other wagons
Avoid:
- A subject that cannot perform the action, or an action that does not serve the goal
- Outcomes that are not a measurable result of the action
- Context that does not make sense for when/where the action occurs
- Listing as produce an artifact not owned by this wagon
- Consuming an artifact owned by this same wagon
- A wagon with no produce artifact of scope: 'external'
Why: A wagon must be semantically coherent: subject/action/goal align, outcome is measurable, produce is owned, consume is foreign, and at least one produce is external.
