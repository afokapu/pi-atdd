Title: Cover every acceptance commit-failure cause in the train
Given: A train whose flow reaches the acceptance commit at the Acceptance Authority boundary
When: the planner authors or revises the train's dispatch graph and acceptances
Then: Each of the four platform:acceptance:commit-failure.cause values (version_conflict, authorization_refused, idempotency_conflict, authority_unavailable) is reachable, registered in the dispatch graph, resolvable, and terminating.
Prefer:
- Plan handling per cause: for each cause confirm its path (separate or shared) is registered, reachable, resolvable and terminating.
- Treat commit-failure coverage as extending the train's coverage up to the Acceptance Authority boundary.
Avoid:
- Covering only some of the four causes, or folding them into an unregistered generic failure path.
- Paths that are registered but unreachable, reachable but unresolved, or that never terminate.
- Deferrals without a tracking reference or expiry used to make coverage look complete.
Unless:
- A specific cause has an explicit tracked-and-expiring deferral (excuses that cause only).
Why: Acceptance commits can fail for four distinct reasons at the Acceptance Authority boundary; any cause that is unreachable, unregistered, unresolvable or non-terminating can strand the train.
