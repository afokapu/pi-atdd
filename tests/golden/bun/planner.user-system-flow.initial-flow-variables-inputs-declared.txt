Title: Declare every initial Cargo input on the train
Given: A train definition for which any artifact may be seeded into Cargo before the first wagon step, whether or not a wagon reads it.
When: The planner authors or edits the train.
Then: Declare every artifact Cargo may be seeded with under inputs.caller (caller-provided) or inputs.spec_materialized (deterministically materialized from the spec).
Prefer:
- Classify each initial artifact explicitly: caller-provided goes in inputs.caller; anything derived deterministically from the spec goes in inputs.spec_materialized.
- Mark which initial inputs are required so missing ones can be rejected before step 1.
Avoid:
- Relying on an undeclared artifact being present in Cargo at train start; undeclared initial inputs are rejected before step 1.
- Leaving a required initial input unsupplied or unmaterialized; missing required initial inputs are rejected before step 1.
- Placing non-deterministic or caller-supplied data under inputs.spec_materialized.
Why: Cargo is seeded and validated before the first wagon step, so the journey can only start from declared initial inputs; undeclared inputs or missing required initial inputs must fail fast before step 1 rather than mid-sequence.
