Title: Give every concrete telemetry item exactly one owning wagon
Given: A plan contains concrete tracking-plan items, each resolving to a logical telemetry artifact URN telemetry:{theme}:{artifact}.
When: Any concrete tracking-plan item is authored, changed, or evaluated (including full-plan validation of unchanged items).
Then: Ensure each item's logical artifact is declared in exactly one wagon's produce[] telemetry (string or list), and the item's owner equals that wagon's slug.
Prefer:
- Declare the logical artifact on the producing wagon's produce[] entry (telemetry: telemetry:{theme}:{artifact}, contract may be null), then set the item's owner to that wagon's slug.
- Decide ownership in the plan first; treat the registry only as something that resolves against plan-declared ownership.
Avoid:
- Concrete items whose artifact no wagon's produce[] declares (emitted by code no plan stands behind).
- Declaring the same logical artifact in two wagons' produce[] (two authors, no arbiter).
- An owner field that names a wagon other than the declaring one, or relying on the registry to grant ownership.
- Treating a wagon-level duplicate-producer finding (planner profile) as covering or exempting the item-level check; both apply.
Why: Two producers make an emission contract a coincidence waiting to diverge; zero means no wagon stands behind the observable outcome. Both are plan defects, caught at the item.
