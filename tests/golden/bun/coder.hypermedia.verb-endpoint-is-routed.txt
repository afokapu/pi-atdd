Title: Route every mutating htmx endpoint from the Station Master
Given: A repo whose Station Master (the module declaring JOURNEY_MAP) resolves, with markup anywhere (changed or not) carrying hx-post/put/patch/delete with a static path
When: the Station Master resolves; every such endpoint repo-wide is checked, not only edited files
Then: Ensure every such endpoint is served (routed) by the Station Master, or drop the affordance.
Prefer:
- Add a Station Master handler matching the endpoint's method and path that checks authorization (401 on failure) and returns the fragment.
- If the journey does not admit the mutation, remove the affordance from markup.
Avoid:
- Adding a mutating button/form to markup ahead of its handler (the common cause).
- Markup minting a state-advancing route the journey never admitted (a parallel, private route space); confirm, indicator and a passing test do not make it routed.
- Removing/renaming a Station Master route while markup still posts to it.
- Merely mentioning the endpoint's path text in the Station Master without a handler that serves that method and path.
Unless:
- hx-get: fetching a fragment is presentation, not a journey.
- Interpolated paths: skipped, not guessed (statically unresolvable bindings stay unjudged).
- No Station Master surface resolves: NOT_APPLICABLE.
Why: htmx declares its route space in markup; unbound, markup mints state-advancing routes the journey never admitted and affordances post into nothing. Mutations advance state, as journeys do, so they owe a route; reads do not.
