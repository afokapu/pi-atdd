Title: Compose every interlocking into a journey
Given: A plan that declares one or more interlockings (regardless of what was edited).
When: The plan is validated at CONFIRM, where any finding of this rule blocks CONFIRM (confirm-blocking).
Then: Declare at least one journey and make every interlocking reached by a journey: as a journey's entrypoint interlocking or as the destination of a continuation reachable from that entrypoint.
Prefer:
- For each interlocking, state the journey that starts it (what triggers the routing decision) or the continuation that leads into it from a reachable interlocking.
- Decide the end-to-end flow: for each interlocking, state what happens after its train completes via continuations.
Avoid:
- Stopping the decomposition at interlockings so it looks complete while the end-to-end flow was never decided.
- A plan with interlockings and no journey (vacuous topology), relying on continuation-closure passing vacuously.
- Orphan interlockings: routing decisions with no stated trigger and no stated place in the product flow.
Why: The journey is where the plan says what starts a routing decision and what happens after its train completes. planner.journey.continuation-closure only validates the journeys a plan declares, so plans with none, or with interlockings outside every journey, would pass it vacuously.
