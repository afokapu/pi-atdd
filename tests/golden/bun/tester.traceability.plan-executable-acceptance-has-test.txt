Title: Bind every declared acceptance to at least one Bun test
Given: A plan declares one or more acceptances
When: the planner declares, edits or keeps an acceptance in the plan (at any phase; the closure validator checks this whenever it runs)
Then: Every declared acceptance is bound by at least one Bun test.
Why: An acceptance with no bound test cannot be executed or checked, so the plan claims behaviour that nothing proves.
