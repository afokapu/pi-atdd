Title: Use a dot for lateral variation in artifact names
Given: A planner is authoring or renaming an artifact name.
When: The name expresses a lateral variation: the same conceptual level as its aspect, differing in flavor, state, type or facet.
Then: Mark the lateral variation with a dot: append the variant to the aspect name after a dot (aspect.variant).
Prefer:
- Follow the illustrative pattern domain:aspect.variant, e.g. domain:aspect.variantA vs domain:aspect.variantB for two states of one aspect.
- Use the dot suffix for a different flavor, state, type or facet of the same aspect.
Avoid:
- Using a dot for something that is not at the same conceptual level (a deeper or different aspect).
- Encoding a lateral variant with another separator or by inventing a new aspect name, which hides that the artifacts are variants of one aspect.
Why: A dot signals that artifacts are variants of one aspect, so readers and tooling can tell flavor/state variants apart from different aspects or levels.
