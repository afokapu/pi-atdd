Title: Default to reference execution: follow declared routes and continuations
Given: A journey declares interlocking routes and train continuations.
When: You plan or document how the journey executes.
Then: Follow the declared routes and train continuations without additional scheduling or speculative transformations.
Prefer:
- Choose reference when ordering matters, the workload is small, behavior must be reproduced, or performance evidence does not justify an alternative.
- Treat reference as the default, not a mandated slow path.
Avoid:
- Planning reference execution as a new runtime dispatch option.
- Inferring unimplemented orchestration behavior from this pattern.
Why: Reference is the default strategy, and it is what the current single-route continuation model supports.
