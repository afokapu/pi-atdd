Title: Route each acceptance to a layer plane by its keyword cues
Given: An acceptance criterion is being assigned or reassigned to a layer plane
When: The plane is chosen for that acceptance
Then: Choose the plane (presentation, application, domain, integration) that the acceptance's keyword cues indicate in the plane keyword table.
Prefer:
- presentation cues: display, visible, screen, render, animation, button, form, modal.
- application cues: workflow, orchestrate, use case, policy, state, saga.
- domain cues: rule, validate, aggregate, entity, invariant, business.
- integration cues: database, persist, external, queue, api, webhook, repository.
Avoid:
- Assigning a plane that contradicts the acceptance's keyword cues (e.g. putting a 'persist' or 'webhook' acceptance in presentation).
- Rewording an acceptance just to hit or avoid a keyword so that a preferred plane gets selected.
Why: The keyword-derived plane drives the downstream plane-to-layer-sequence routing. A wrong plane sends the work to the wrong layers.
