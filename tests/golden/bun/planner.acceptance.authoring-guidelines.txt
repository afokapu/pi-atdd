Title: Bind each acceptance to exactly one metric_id
Given: A planner is authoring or editing an acceptance or WMBT, or placing/moving a WMBT's acceptances, tests or artifacts.
When: The plan artifact, test or artifact is written or moved.
Then: Bind each acceptance to exactly one metric_id from the dimension->metric_id table in planner.criteria.metric-mapping, matching its parent WMBT's dimension.
Prefer:
- Take the metric_id from the metric-mapping row for the parent WMBT's dimension.
- Write a separate acceptance for each additional measure.
- Mandatory on every WMBT create/edit: declare exactly one dimension and one lens_ref.
- Mandatory on every placement/move: put the WMBT, its acceptances, tests and artifacts in the WMBT's own location, so history and review of the WMBT stay in one place.
Avoid:
- Mixed measures: one acceptance binding more than one metric_id.
- A metric_id not in the metric-mapping table.
- A WMBT without exactly one dimension and one lens_ref.
- A WMBT's acceptances, tests or artifacts placed apart from the WMBT.
Why: Keeping the WMBT, its acceptances, tests and artifacts together keeps their history and review cohesive.
