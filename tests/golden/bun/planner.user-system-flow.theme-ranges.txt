Title: Declare named themes on every train; keep them out of the train id
Given: A train exists in, or is being added to, the plan.
When: The planner authors, edits or reviews that train.
Then: Declare one or more named themes on the train, using the repository-defined theme taxonomy (source: plan/_themes.yaml).
Prefer:
- Treat themes as named plan metadata that classifies the journey the train covers.
- Keep the train identity in the typed form train:{subject}:{slug}.
- Take theme values from the taxonomy as defined: it reserves zero as commons (reserved_zero = commons) and other values are repository-defined; do not infer further semantics for the reserved value.
Avoid:
- Encoding a theme in the train identity (e.g. a theme number or theme prefix in the id); digits that do not encode a theme are not affected.
- Reviving the retired numeric theme-range scheme to derive or reserve train ids.
- Leaving a train with no declared theme.
Why: Themes classify the journey, while identity is the typed train:{subject}:{slug}; keeping them separate means classification is metadata and is never encoded in identity.
