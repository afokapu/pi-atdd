Title: Store appendices in the appendices/ subdirectory of the artifact they describe
Given: An appendix (mockup, diagram or supplementary file) describes a wagon, WMBT, feature or component, wherever it is currently stored.
When: You plan, add, move, rename, edit or review it, or come across it.
Then: Store the appendix in the appendices/ subdirectory of the artifact it describes, at that level's path: wagon {output_dir}/wagons/{wagon-name}/appendices/; wmbt {output_dir}/wagons/{wagon-name}/plan/{wmbt-id}/appendices/; feature {output_dir}/wagons/{wagon-name}/features/{feature-name}/appendices/; component {output_dir}/wagons/{wagon-name}/features/{feature-name}/components/{component-name}/appendices/.
Prefer:
- Use the most specific artifact level the appendix describes, and create its appendices/ if missing.
- Move any misplaced appendix you find there, including ones in shared folders.
Avoid:
- Placing an appendix directly in the artifact directory (e.g. wagons/{wagon-name}/{file}).
- Storing it at another artifact level or in a shared central folder.
Why: Co-located appendices keep artifacts self-contained and make supplementary content discoverable relative to the artifact.
