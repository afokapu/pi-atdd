Title: Prefix a themed wagon's produced URNs with the wagon theme
Given: A wagon declares a theme and produces artifacts, contracts, or telemetry identities (newly authored or already existing, declared in any location).
When: The planner authors, edits, validates, or reviews that wagon or any of its produced identities; theme or URN edits are examples, not the only trigger.
Then: Make the leading theme segment of every produced artifact, contract, and telemetry URN equal the wagon's declared theme.
Avoid:
- Reusing another theme's prefix for a produced URN because the artifact feels related to that domain.
- Treating this namespace check as a statement about runtime module ownership, agent roles, or domain vocabulary.
- Changing the wagon's theme just to make a mismatched URN pass instead of fixing the URN namespace.
Unless:
- The wagon declares no theme: it is not subject to this cross-field predicate.
Why: The theme token namespaces produced URNs; this is namespace consistency only, so a mismatched prefix breaks the identity namespace without implying anything about ownership, roles, or vocabulary.
