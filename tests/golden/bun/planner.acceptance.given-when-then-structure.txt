Title: Structure every acceptance as Given/When/Then
Given: You are authoring or revising an acceptance.
When: You write a new acceptance or change any of its sections (given, when or then — one is enough).
Then: Give the acceptance a required `when` declaring the single harness-specific trigger and a required `then` that is an array of assertions, with `given` declaring only the required preconditions.
Prefer:
- Express required preconditions in `given` using the listed precondition fields — auth, builders, time, stubbing, knobs — and include a precondition only when the scenario needs it.
- Shape `when` by harness: http → method/path; unit → target; event → topic; ws/e2e → interactions.
- Use only allowed assertion kinds in `then`: status, body, headers, error, event_published, state_changed, metric.
Avoid:
- Omitting `when` or `then` (both REQUIRED).
- More than one trigger in `when`, or a trigger not expressed in its harness-specific shape.
- Putting into `given` preconditions the scenario does not require.
- `then` that is not an array, or assertions using a kind outside the allowed list.
Why: A uniform, minimal Given/When/Then shape lets each acceptance be executed by its harness and checked mechanically: one trigger keeps the scenario unambiguous, typed assertions keep outcomes verifiable.
