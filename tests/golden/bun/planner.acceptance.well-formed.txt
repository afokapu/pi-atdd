Title: Give every acceptance Given/When/Then prose and a verifiable outcome
Given: A WMBT node in a plan declares one or more acceptances.
When: The planner authors or edits an acceptance under that WMBT, or CONFIRM validates every declared acceptance.
Then: Each acceptance has present, non-empty Given/When/Then abstract prose, and at least one Then line states a verifiable outcome a generated test can assert.
Prefer:
- Write Given as the preconditions, When as the trigger action, and Then as the expected observable outcomes.
- Phrase at least one Then line as a concrete, assertable observable outcome.
Avoid:
- Leaving given, when or then abstract missing or empty.
- Using a bare placeholder token (TBD, TODO, N/A) as the outcome — it is not a verifiable outcome.
- Filling the fields with non-empty filler to pass the presence check while stating no assertable outcome.
- Treating a well-formed acceptance as proof the WMBT's acceptance set is complete — this rule checks local shape only.
Why: An acceptance whose narrative is missing, empty, or states no assertable outcome cannot be turned into a test, so its WMBT is unverifiable in practice while appearing covered on paper.
