Title: Feedback-loop features must close the loop in SMOKE
Given: A feature is marked `kind: feedback-loop`.
When: The planner authors or revises its WMBTs and SMOKE acceptances.
Then: At least one SMOKE acceptance must declare a `close_the_loop:` block carrying both `consumer_reacted` (the consumer-side assertion the test makes) and `drift_resolved` (the convergence assertion).
Prefer:
- Add (or extend) a SMOKE acceptance on the feature's primary WMBT with a close_the_loop: block declaring consumer_reacted and drift_resolved.
- Phrase them so the test asserts the consumer actually received the correction and a producer re-run yields 0 new corrections for the same drift.
Avoid:
- Producer-only SMOKE assertions (artifact existence, schema validation) as the sole SMOKE coverage.
- A close_the_loop: block with only one of the two keys.
- consumer_reacted satisfied by a fake send-history.
Unless:
- The kind: line carries the dated hatch `kind: feedback-loop  # atdd:suppress(planner.smoke.feedback-loop-close-the-loop) UNTIL=YYYY-MM-DD`; dated, so the debt expires.
Why: Producer-only smoke assertions give false-positive green CI while the loop's consumer end is never exercised.
