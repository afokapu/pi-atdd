Title: Keep every plan artifact parseable
Given: The repository has .yaml, .yml or .json artifacts under plan/ (outside vendored or generated trees).
When: The plan tree is in scope for planning work or validation; this covers every such artifact, changed or unchanged.
Then: Every in-scope plan artifact parses as YAML or JSON, including files you did not change.
Prefer:
- Fix the syntax the finding reports.
- Treat parsing as the first gate: schema and identity checks only run once the artifact parses.
Avoid:
- Leaving a plan artifact half-written or unparseable and relying on later rules to catch its other defects; they cannot see it.
- Silencing the finding by renaming, moving the file into a vendored/generated tree, or changing its extension instead of fixing the syntax.
Unless:
- Vendored and generated trees (node_modules, dist, build, _generated) are never scanned.
Why: An artifact that does not parse is invisible to every other rule, so its defects hide behind one error.
