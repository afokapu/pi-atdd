Title: Keep every plan identity unique
Given: the scanned plan artifacts, each declaring an identity (train_id, interlocking_id, journey_id, urn, …)
When: the plan is authored or validated — whether an artifact is being created, copied, renamed, or a duplicate already exists
Then: ensure no two plan artifacts declare the same identity.
Prefer:
- When a duplicate is found, rename one of the artifacts, or merge them.
- Check the existing plan artifacts for the identity before declaring it in a new or copied artifact.
Avoid:
- Copying an artifact as a template and leaving its identity unchanged.
- Keeping two artifacts with the same identity and relying on whichever is read first to win.
- Silencing the finding by moving a plan artifact into a vendored or generated tree.
Unless:
- The artifacts live in vendored or generated trees (node_modules, dist, build, _generated), which are never scanned.
Why: A reference to a duplicated identity resolves to whichever file was read first, so references become ambiguous and order-dependent.
