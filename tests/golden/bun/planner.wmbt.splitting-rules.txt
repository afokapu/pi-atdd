Title: Split WMBTs that exceed 5 acceptances or mix concerns (advisory)
Given: You are authoring or revising a WMBT and its acceptances. This is advisory guidance, not schema-enforced.
When: The WMBT has more than 5 acceptances, or its statement mentions multiple dimensions, involves different actors+actions, or combines divergent gates (ALL vs ANY).
Then: Split it into separate WMBTs so none exceeds 5 acceptances and none mixes dimensions, different actors+actions, or ALL/ANY gates.
Prefer:
- Treat more than 5 acceptances as a signal of mixed concerns and split by concern.
- Split along every trigger present: separate WMBTs per dimension, per distinct actor+action pairing, and per gate type (ALL vs ANY); re-check each resulting WMBT against all triggers.
- If an over-cap WMBT is one truth across UI/API/DB (gate ALL) or one coherent sequence, flag the cap-vs-keep-together conflict for review instead of silently choosing; the source does not define precedence.
Avoid:
- Packing more than 5 acceptances into one WMBT.
- A WMBT statement that mentions multiple dimensions.
- Mixing ALL-gated and ANY-gated acceptances in one WMBT.
- Splitting only to get under the cap while the resulting WMBTs still mix concerns, or splitting one truth or one sequence apart.
Unless:
- single_truth_multiple_layers: the acceptances assert the same outcome at UI/API/DB with gate ALL — keep together.
- coherent_sequence: the acceptances are sequential steps of one process — keep together.
Why: Beyond 5 acceptances, or mixed dimensions, different actors+actions, or divergent gates, indicate mixed concerns that should be separate WMBTs.
