Title: Keep each wagon at least as cohesive internally as coupled to any single neighbor wagon (advisory)
Given: A wagon (at or above the min-graph-size guard) whose WMBTs, connected when they share >= min_shared salient object_of_control tokens, form one undirected graph with neighboring wagons' WMBTs
When: You author or revise the wagon's WMBTs, allocate WMBTs across wagons, or review existing wagons
Then: Plan the wagon so its cohesion (intra-wagon WMBT edges) is >= its coupling (cross-wagon edges) to every single neighbor wagon; otherwise expect an advisory [MERGE] signal.
Prefer:
- When a wagon is more bound to one neighbor than to itself, treat the [MERGE] signal as an advisory hint to consider merging it with that neighbor.
- Judge cohesion and coupling on the same WMBT shared-token graph so the two counts are commensurable.
Avoid:
- Treating a [MERGE] finding as blocking or gating the plan — it is advisory only and never blocks.
- Flagging wagons below the min-graph-size guard on account of separability.
- Rewording object_of_control tokens merely to dodge shared-token edges instead of reconsidering the wagon boundary.
Unless:
- The wagon is below the configured min-graph-size guard: it is skipped, not flagged.
Why: A wagon should be a separable unit of work; if it is more bound to one neighbor than to itself, its boundary may be wrong, so the advisory signal suggests considering a merge with its tightest neighbor.
