Title: Compose WMBT statements from the template
Given: A WMBT has a chosen direction, dimension, object of control and optional context clarifier.
When: The planner writes or revises the WMBT statement.
Then: Compose the statement exactly as `{direction} {dimension} of {object_of_control} [context_clarifier]`, i.e. `${direction} ${dimension} of ${object_of_control}${context_clarifier ? ' ' + context_clarifier : ''}`.
Prefer:
- Derive the statement from the WMBT's direction, dimension and object-of-control values so the text and the parts stay consistent.
- Append the context clarifier after a single space only when one exists; otherwise end at the object of control.
Avoid:
- Free-form statements that skip, reorder or rephrase the template parts (e.g. omitting the literal `of`).
- Trailing spaces or placeholder text when no context clarifier is set.
Why: A fixed composition makes every WMBT statement reproducible from its direction, dimension, object of control and clarifier, so statements stay comparable and machine-checkable.
