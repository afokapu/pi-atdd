Title: Resolve every interlocking participant and lifeline
Given: A plan contains an interlocking that lists participants and lifelines
When: The plan is authored, revised or checked
Then: Every participant and lifeline the interlocking lists is either an external actor (`user:…`, `system:…`) or a typed reference to an artifact the plan declares.
Prefer:
- Declare the missing participant as an artifact in the plan and reference it with a typed reference.
- Correct a lifeline that points at the wrong, an undeclared, or an untyped target.
Avoid:
- Routing decisions between participants the plan does not declare.
- Free-text or untyped participant/lifeline names, even when a matching artifact exists.
Why: A routing decision between participants the plan does not declare cannot be traced to anything that implements it.
