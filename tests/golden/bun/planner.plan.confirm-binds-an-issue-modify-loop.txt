Title: Re-open the plan session when a post-commit validator fails
Given: A plan was ratified and its substrate committed.
When: A post-commit validator fails.
Then: Re-open the plan session, adjust the plan with the operator, and re-author it.
Prefer:
- Make the adjustment together with the operator inside the re-opened session.
Avoid:
- Leaving the validator failure unresolved instead of re-opening the session for adjustment and re-author.
Why: The session's modify loop is how plan defects caught after commit are corrected: through operator adjustment and re-author.
