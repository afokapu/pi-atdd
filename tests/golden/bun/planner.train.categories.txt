Title: Classify each train with its category field
Given: A planner is authoring or editing a train, including its steps, acceptance or outcome
When: the train is created, named or renamed, or its scenario semantics change in a way that may alter its category
Then: Set the train's `category` field to exactly one of nominal, error, alternate or exception, chosen by the category definitions.
Prefer:
- nominal: the intended journey; the domain runs to completion and returns the outcome the job exists to produce.
- alternate: another in-domain route to success (input mode, channel, configuration, persona); a success, not a failure.
- exception: the domain ran and refused (negative verdict, the business rule working); retry is refused identically (validation, rule violations, policy denials).
- error: fault outside the domain; domain never ran, no verdict or postcondition; retry may succeed once the fault clears (crashes, collaborator unavailability, infrastructure).
- Reclassify by editing the category field, without a category-driven rename.
Avoid:
- Encoding the category in the train's name or id (category is metadata, not identity).
- Labelling a business-rule refusal as error, or an alternate success as a failure.
Why: Category is metadata, not identity, so a train is reclassified by changing a field rather than its name; the closed set keeps success, in-domain refusal and out-of-domain fault distinct.
