Title: Advance decomposition only through per-candidate keep/pivot/kill verdicts
Given: A decomposition session where the planner proposes candidates (heuristic, analog, main job, wagon, feature, WMBT, acceptance, etc.).
When: The planner would advance past a candidate or act on it.
Then: Advance each candidate only on the operator's individual keep, pivot or kill verdict for that candidate.
Prefer:
- Keep: accept as-is and proceed.
- Pivot: apply the operator's named modification, then re-confirm.
- Kill: reject; if all candidates at a granularity are killed, re-propose.
- Write an artifact only after a keep at a final granularity (wagon, feature, WMBT, train, interlocking, contract, acceptance).
- Heuristic, analog, main job are reasoning granularities: real candidates, but a keep records the decision and writes no artifact.
Avoid:
- Advancing a candidate that has no individual verdict (several individually assigned verdicts may be delivered together).
- Writing an artifact before a final-granularity keep, or a pivot before re-confirmation.
- Erroring on a kept reasoning-level candidate, or recording kill when the operator kept it.
Why: Per-unit verdicts keep the operator in control and recorded decisions truthful; only final granularities map to an author writer.
