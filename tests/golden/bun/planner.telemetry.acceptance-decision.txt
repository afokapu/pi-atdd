Title: Every acceptance makes an explicit telemetry decision
Given: Telemetry is adopted: a telemetry root exists, or any acceptance declares telemetry (either alone suffices).
When: You plan or review any acceptance or tracking-plan item.
Then: Every acceptance declares disposition `required` listing at least one well-formed concrete telemetry URN that the acceptance demands be observed emitted and that exists as a tracking-plan item under the telemetry root, or disposition `not-applicable` with a rationale of at least 20 characters.
Prefer:
- Shape: `telemetry: {disposition: required, events: [<concrete telemetry:event:… URN from the tracking plan>]}` or `telemetry: {disposition: not-applicable, rationale: <considered reason, ≥20 chars>}`.
- List required items by kind (events, metrics, traces, logs).
- Treat not-applicable as a considered answer, not the absence of one.
- Make every acceptance a tracking-plan item names resolve to a declared acceptance.
Avoid:
- Omitting the telemetry block once adopted — silence is not a disposition.
- Malformed, non-concrete or unplanned URNs (the requirement is unplannable).
- Token rationales under 20 characters that are not a real answer.
- Requiring a test for every debug log or internal span.
- Dangling tracking-plan acceptance references.
- Forcing acceptance lists and plan references to mirror each other.
Unless:
- Diagnostic logs and internal spans are not automatically telemetry obligations; the decision is per acceptance.
- Inert until adoption: no telemetry root and no acceptance telemetry declaration → nothing emitted (see planner.telemetry.tracking-plan-schema).
Why: An explicit per-acceptance decision removes the false choice between testing every debug log and having no observability requirement; both-direction resolution keeps traceability intact.
