Title: Bind every guarded alt/opt fragment to acceptances, and back
Given: An interlocking contains guarded alt/opt fragments or flow-branch acceptances (changed or not)
When: The planner authors or edits the interlocking, or the plan reaches CONFIRM
Then: Bind both ways: every guarded alt/opt fragment references at least one acceptance, and every flow-branch acceptance references back the guard/fragment it covers.
Prefer:
- Give fragments and guards stable ids and list acceptance refs on each fragment
- On each flow-branch acceptance, cite the guard_id/fragment_id it covers
Avoid:
- Guarded alt/opt fragments with no acceptance
- Flow-branch acceptances bound to no guard or fragment
- One-way binding: refs on fragments without back-references, or the reverse
- Binding a fragment to an unrelated or placeholder acceptance just to pass the check
Why: Bidirectional binding keeps branches traceable. Each guarded fragment points to its acceptances, and each branch acceptance points to the fragment it covers.
