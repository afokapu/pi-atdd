Title: Give planner artifacts stable URN-based names matching the registered grammar
Given: A planner artifact (for example a plan, wagon, WMBT, acceptance or planning doc; the list is not exhaustive)
When: you author, name, review or change it — or it simply exists in the repository
Then: The artifact carries a stable URN-based name that matches the registered grammar and stays constant across renames/refactors.
Prefer:
- Look up the repository's registered naming grammar before naming, build the name as a URN from it (a stable_name), and check the name parses against that grammar.
- Keep the same URN when the artifact is renamed, moved or refactored.
Avoid:
- Free-form or ad-hoc names that do not match the registered grammar.
- Changing an artifact's URN as part of a rename or refactor.
Why: The convention defines a stable_name as URN-based, constant across renames/refactors and conforming to the registered grammar; planner artifacts must carry such a name.
