Title: Name every wagon verb-object
Given: A plan contains a wagon slug that will pass through CONFIRM
When: The planner chooses or keeps the wagon slug (checked at CONFIRM, confirm-blocking)
Then: The wagon slug is verb-object: kebab-case, at least two tokens, the leading token a verb from the convention verb lexicon, and no connective tokens.
Prefer:
- Lead with an action verb from the verb lexicon at conventions:planner:feature#/artifact_seeds/verb_selection, then the object it acts on (e.g. resolve-dilemmas, manage-users, route-request, preview-changes, respond-event)
- When a wagon slug is a brand or proper-noun name, list it explicitly in wagon.convention.yaml verb_object_enforcement.brand_exceptions
Avoid:
- Noun-first or object-verb slugs such as mode-select
- Single-token slugs such as blitz
- Connective tokens in the slug (e.g. and/or/of-style joiners)
- Treating a brand or proper-noun slug as implicitly exempt — an unlisted exception is a violation
- Adding an ordinary (non-brand, non-proper-noun) slug to brand_exceptions to bypass the rule
Unless:
- The slug is a brand or proper-noun name listed explicitly in wagon.convention.yaml verb_object_enforcement.brand_exceptions
Why: A verb-object slug states what the wagon does, keeping wagon names uniform and aligned with the shared verb lexicon; explicit brand exceptions keep deviations visible and reviewable.
