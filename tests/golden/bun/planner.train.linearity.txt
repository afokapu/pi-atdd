Title: Keep train sequences strictly linear; model new paths as new trains
Given: You are planning a train
When: you author or change any train sequence (e.g. adding steps, or when the flow seems to need a loop, a route, a branch or an alternative path)
Then: Keep the train's sequence strictly linear (steps only, no loops, routes or branching) and express every new path as a new train.
Prefer:
- Model each new or alternative path as its own new linear train instead of a loop, route or branch inside the existing sequence
- Number steps sequentially with no gaps (1, 2, 3, …)
- Give every step exactly one from and exactly one to
Avoid:
- Adding loop or route sequence elements to a train
- Encoding branching through a step with multiple from/to targets
- Leaving gaps in step numbering after inserting or removing steps
Why: Linearity is the no-loops/no-routes/no-branching rule for train sequences: sequences contain steps only, and new paths MUST become new trains rather than variations inside one sequence.
