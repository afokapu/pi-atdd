Title: Component URN casing: wagon/feature kebab-lower, name facets kebab-lower or CamelCase
Given: A component URN is involved, in any artifact
When: you write, copy or reference it
Then: {wagon} and {feature} must be kebab-lower. {name} is a logical component name (PascalCase, camelCase, or dot-separated), and each '.'-separated facet must be kebab-lower or CamelCase.
Avoid:
- A wagon or feature that is not kebab-lower, e.g. component:Resolve-Dilemmas:choose-option:OptionValidator:backend:domain.
Why: Consistent casing lets the graph address components unambiguously and case-sensitively.
