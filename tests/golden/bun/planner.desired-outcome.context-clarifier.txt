Title: Give every WMBT a one-line context clarifier
Given: A WMBT is being authored or edited
When: the planner writes its definition
Then: Declare a context clarifier: a detailed situational phrase, one line at most, that says when, where or how the WMBT applies. It is required.
Prefer:
- One concrete line that names the situation the WMBT applies to (when, where or how), so the statement template can append it to the WMBT statement.
Avoid:
- Leaving the context clarifier out or empty.
- Clarifiers that span more than one line or turn into a paragraph.
- Vague or generic qualifiers that do not narrow the situation.
Why: The statement template appends the clarifier to the WMBT statement. Without a precise, situational one-line qualifier, the WMBT does not say when it applies.
