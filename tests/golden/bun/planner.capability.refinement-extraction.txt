Title: Extract all refinement fields when refining ambiguous input into a wagon
Given: A planner holds ambiguous input (possibly free-form) that must become a structured wagon.
When: Refining that input into a wagon definition.
Then: Extract subject, action, goal, context, outcome, produce, consume and theme into the wagon definition.
Prefer:
- subject: WHO/WHAT performs the action (actor:role or entity:state).
- action: core present-tense verb phrase.
- goal: WHY/motivation, single intent statement.
- context: WHEN/WHERE, comma-separated conditions.
- outcome: measurable progress/result statement.
- produce: owned output artifacts; consume: input artifacts owned by other wagons.
- theme: one of mechanic, scenario, match, sensory, player, league, audience, monetization, partnership, commons — pick the one whose scope matches (scopes in shared:context.planner.wagon-theme-taxonomy).
- description: at least 10 characters.
Avoid:
- Leaving a refinement field unextracted.
- Non-present-tense actions; goals with several intents; unmeasurable outcomes.
- Other wagons' artifacts under produce, or own artifacts under consume.
- Themes outside the taxonomy; descriptions under 10 characters.
Why: Structured fields make the wagon's intent, scope, ownership and success checkable; later coherence checks validate exactly these fields.
