Title: Cached or incremental results: plan reuse acceptances and hit-rate measurement
Given: Work reuses or incrementally recomputes an expensive, repeatable read or derivation with stable identities and tractable dependencies
When: writing its acceptances or tests
Then: Include tests for cold and warm paths, staleness, invalidation races, tenant isolation and equivalence with the accepted result, and measure hit rate.
Prefer:
- Equivalence acceptances that compare cached/incremental output with the accepted (uncached) result.
Avoid:
- Treating hit rate as proof of correctness without equivalence and staleness tests.
Why: Reuse is only trustworthy if tests show warm results equal accepted results, stay fresh and stay isolated.
