Title: Collaborate through durable accountable delegation; mutate only under an assigned task
Given: A workflow agent in the delegation chain operator → main → named coordinator → driver works within its assigned project and task.
When: It delegates, messages, escalates, manages seats, inbox, merges or projections, or makes any code/repository mutation.
Then: An existing assigned task in the same project must exist before any code or repository mutation; the task defines, assigns, executes, evidences and completes that work.
Prefer:
- Proactively dispatch safe independent work; verify durable delivery and visible execution, distinguishing persisted, delivered, acknowledged, awaiting actor, executing.
- Escalate material blockers upward with exact evidence, economical recommendation, viable alternatives and tradeoffs, and safe work that can continue.
- Resolve assumptions or disagreement by time-boxed two-way evidence dialogue with the responsible actor; escalate only unresolved decisions.
- Atomic assignment; unassigned work gets a project-main decision proposal: purpose, priority, recommended assignee, alternatives, consequences, dependencies, safe work meanwhile.
- As a coordinating actor, reconcile the durable inbox as the first action at every session, turn and task resumption, and after every bounded action, phase completion or wait before the next action.
- Promptly complete, supersede, reject or time-bound defer work; post decisions and open questions as messages when they happen.
- Manage main, coordinator and driver seats only via atdd-flow commands (spawn, bind, pi runtime launch, task lifecycle, handover).
- Keep the full policy only in this convention; managed AGENTS.md/CLAUDE.md blocks hold only its canonical pointer, and updating them preserves all user-authored content outside the marked block.
- As operator, accept and receipt inbound communication from any seat.
Avoid:
- Messages for anything but communication, evidence, consensus, authorization or status; requesting executable work in a message.
- Inferring authorization; restarting productive sessions merely to relay policy.
- Next-task discovery outside the same project; cross-project dialogue without a real dependency/interface or bypassing counterpart authority.
- More than one bounded active task or worktree per driver; folding adjacent work in instead of assigning it separately.
- Letting an actionable expects_result leave view before it is answered, executed, delegated with verified delivery and follow-up, or explicitly deferred, superseded or closed.
- Operator sending substantive direction, results or wakes to any seat but main@project.
- Anyone but main@project merging its repository; the operator may authorize, relay or inspect but never merges.
- Hand-managing seats; skipping lifecycle steps or gates; duplicative or non-minimal implementations.
- Dropping or rewriting user-authored content outside the marked block when updating a managed AGENTS.md/CLAUDE.md block.
Unless:
- A message may request executable work when it references an existing assigned task.
- A seat may be started, stopped, rebound or succeeded by hand under explicit owner direction, completing the supported bind/reconciliation immediately after.
Why: Accountable delegation, evidence-based escalation, economical delivery and bounded two-way resolution keep work durable, visible and within its assigned project and task.
