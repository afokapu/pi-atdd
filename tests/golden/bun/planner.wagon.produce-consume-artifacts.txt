Title: Give every wagon produce[] and consume[] entry its required fields
Given: A wagon definition with produce[] and/or consume[] artifact lists
When: The planner adds or edits a produce[] or consume[] entry
Then: Every produce[] entry declares name, contract and telemetry (optional: to, urn, version), and every consume[] entry declares name (optional: from, contract, telemetry, urn, version).
Prefer:
- Write the contract and telemetry keys on every produce[] entry, even when their values are null as in the source's internal-artifact example (to: internal)
- Give produce[].to only internal or external; omitting it means external
Avoid:
- Leaving out contract or telemetry keys on a produce[] entry
- A consume[] entry with no name
- Fields other than the listed required/optional ones on produce[] or consume[] entries
- A from value outside wagon:<slug>|system:<service>|appendix:<type>|internal, or a to value other than internal|external
Why: Produce/consume entries are the declared artifact interface that contract, data-flow, semantic and telemetry-ownership checks resolve against, so their shape must be uniform.
