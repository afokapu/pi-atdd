Title: Declare at least one SMOKE acceptance on every WMBT
Given: Any WMBT in the plan — new, edited, or existing and untouched
When: the planner authors, edits, reviews or validates it
Then: Ensure it declares at least one acceptance whose identity.urn matches acc:<wagon>:<wmbt_id>-SMOKE-NNN[-<slug>] and whose identity.phase is SMOKE.
Prefer:
- Make the SMOKE acceptance the explicit real-infrastructure check between GREEN and REFACTOR.
- Repair: `atdd repo resolve <wmbt-urn>`, open the WMBT YAML at the violation location, add the SMOKE acceptance, re-run the validator until clean.
Avoid:
- WMBTs with no declared real-infrastructure (SMOKE) check.
- Assuming downstream SMOKE tests suffice without declaring the acceptance in the WMBT.
- Suppressing a WMBT that exercises real infrastructure, or without an UNTIL expiry.
Unless:
- WMBT is genuinely docs-only / metadata with no real infra to verify: add `# atdd:suppress(planner.wmbt.must-have-smoke-acceptance) UNTIL=YYYY-MM-DD` on its urn: line, YYYY-MM-DD = today + 90 days.
Why: Tester rules only check coverage of SMOKE tests that exist; without a declared SMOKE acceptance the lifecycle has no explicit real-infrastructure verification before REFACTOR.
