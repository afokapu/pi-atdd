Title: Keep artifact-name hierarchy and variant depth shallow
Given: The planner is choosing or reconsidering an artifact name
When: Deciding how many hierarchy levels and variant (dot) levels the name has
Then: Keep hierarchy at 2-3 levels (4-5 max, sparingly) and variants at 0-1 dots (2 max), with each separator adding clear semantic value.
Prefer:
- Typical hierarchy depth of 2-3 levels
- Typical variant depth of 0-1 levels (dots)
- Remove a separator (and its level) when it does not add clear semantic value
Avoid:
- Hierarchy beyond 5 levels; using 4-5 levels routinely rather than sparingly
- More than 2 variant levels; multiple dots often indicate over-specification
- Any separator that does not add clear semantic value, at any depth
Why: Depth is the recommended nesting per separator; multiple dots often indicate over-specification, so each separator must add clear semantic value.
