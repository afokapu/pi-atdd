Title: Bind wagon produce/consume contracts to registered contract identities
Given: Wagon manifests (plan/<wagon>/_<wagon>.yaml) declare produce/consume entries; contracts/_contracts.yaml is the single source of truth for each contract's identity, path, theme, producers and consumers.
When: You author, change, review or validate produce/consume entries, their contract: fields, or registry entries.
Then: Every non-null contract: URN on a produce or consume entry must, after stripping the contract: prefix, resolve to an identity registered in contracts/_contracts.yaml.
Prefer:
- Add new contract identities to the registry through its authoring flow (create_contract), so the registry entry carries identity, path, theme, producers and consumers.
- Mandatory (NULL-CROSS-WAGON): give the producing entry of every cross-wagon edge (artifact name produced by one wagon, consumed by a different wagon) a non-null registered contract.
- Mandatory (UNCONSUMED): mark a produced non-null contract that no other wagon consumes as to: external.
Avoid:
- URNs whose identity, after stripping the contract: prefix, is absent from the registry (typos, renamed or deleted contracts). A prefixed URN is valid when its stripped identity is a registry key.
- contract: null on a cross-wagon producer (dangling producer, no contract binds the edge) — a required invariant, not optional.
- A produced non-null contract no other wagon consumes and not marked to: external — a required invariant, not optional.
- Silencing findings by nulling contract: or adding to: external to an internally consumed contract.
Unless:
- A produced contract with no consuming wagon is coherent if marked to: external (exempts UNCONSUMED only).
Why: The registry owns contract identity and producer/consumer ownership; unregistered URNs, null-bound cross-wagon edges and orphan producers let plan graph and registry drift.
