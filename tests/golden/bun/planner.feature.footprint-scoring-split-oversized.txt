Title: Split L/XL features; decompose M features when necessary
Given: A feature's footprint bracket is M (11–15), L (16–25) or XL (≥26).
When: The planner finalizes the feature's plan after sizing.
Then: Split every L and XL feature. For an M feature, decompose it when decomposition is necessary.
Prefer:
- For an M feature, actively assess whether decomposition is necessary. The source gives no criterion, so this is a judgment, but it is not a free pass: once necessity is established, decomposing is required.
Avoid:
- Keeping an L or XL feature unsplit.
- Keeping an M feature whole when its decomposition is necessary.
- Retyping or dropping components to get under the split threshold instead of splitting.
Why: Oversized footprints signal features too large to plan and deliver as one unit.
