Title: State an own metric on a WMBT whose pair is deliberately unmapped
Given: A WMBT under plan/ has effort or frequency with direction maximize or increase — a pair the table marks `unmapped`.
When: You create or edit that WMBT.
Then: The WMBT states its own metric instead of inheriting a table default.
Prefer:
- Treat the WMBT as legal: an unmapped pair is a deliberate omission, not an error.
- Remember the synonyms: effort/increase and frequency/increase are unmapped exactly like their maximize forms.
Avoid:
- Inheriting a default — including the dimension's downward metric (metric:user_steps, metric:occurrence_rate) reused for the upward intent.
Why: No upward metric was ever chosen for effort or frequency; their only defaults are downward, so there is nothing valid to inherit.
