Title: Declare the train family that matches its terminal contract
Given: A train definition (new, edited or existing) declares a terminal contract
When: the planner authors, edits, reviews or validates that train definition
Then: Ensure the train declares family:delivery if and only if the terminal requires an Acceptance Authority commit AND the terminal primary is platform:acceptance:commit-receipt, and family:behavior otherwise.
Prefer:
- Decide the terminal contract first, then derive the family from it; the family is controlled redundancy that mirrors the terminal contract.
- When editing a terminal contract, update the family in the same change so the two stay consistent.
Avoid:
- Omitting the family field — a missing family is invalid, not a default.
- Declaring family:delivery when only one of the two conditions holds (Acceptance Authority commit required, primary = platform:acceptance:commit-receipt).
- Treating the family as a second source of truth that overrides or diverges from the terminal contract.
Why: The family is fixed by the terminal contract; keeping it as checked, controlled redundancy (not an independent source of truth) prevents trains being routed or validated under the wrong family.
