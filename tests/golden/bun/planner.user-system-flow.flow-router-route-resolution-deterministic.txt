Title: Declare a deterministic route resolution strategy for every interlocking
Given: A train plan contains an interlocking (whether newly authored, edited, or unchanged)
When: the planner authors or changes it, or the plan is validated at CONFIRM
Then: The interlocking must explicitly declare a deterministic route resolution strategy—fail_on_multiple_match, or first_priority with unique integer priorities on its routes—so that ambiguous matching routes fail closed unless resolved by those declared unique priorities.
Prefer:
- Write the strategy name explicitly on the interlocking, using exactly one of the two allowed strategies.
- When choosing first_priority, give every route a unique integer priority.
Avoid:
- Leaving the strategy implicit or relying on declaration order or any other undeclared tie-break to pick a route.
- Declaring first_priority with duplicate, missing, or non-integer priorities.
- Using strategy names other than fail_on_multiple_match and first_priority.
Why: Route resolution must be explicit and deterministic; when several routes match, the interlocking must fail closed unless a declared unique priority resolves the ambiguity, rather than picking a route arbitrarily.
