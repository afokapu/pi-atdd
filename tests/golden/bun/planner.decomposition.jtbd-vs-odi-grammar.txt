Title: Write the main job in JTBD grammar and WMBTs in ODI grammar
Given: You are decomposing work into a main job and its WMBTs in plan/ artifacts
When: you write the main job statement or a WMBT
Then: State the main job in JTBD job-statement grammar (verb + object + contextual clarifier) and each WMBT in ODI outcome grammar (direction + dimension + object_of_control + context_clarifier), keeping the two grammars separate.
Prefer:
- Main job as a JTBD statement: verb + object + contextual clarifier (e.g. 'Listen to music while commuting').
- WMBTs as ODI outcomes filling the wmbt.schema fields: direction + dimension + object_of_control + context_clarifier.
Avoid:
- Premature collapse: stating the main job as an outcome too early (replacing the job statement with an ODI outcome statement).
- Mixing the grammars: writing WMBTs as job statements instead of ODI outcomes.
Why: Collapsing the job into an outcome too early produces malformed plan/ artifacts, so the two grammars must stay separate.
