Title: Declare acceptance telemetry signals as artifact-based telemetry URNs
Given: An acceptance is being created or revised
When: The planner writes the acceptance and its telemetry signals
Then: Every acceptance declares telemetry signals (REQUIRED) named as artifact-based URNs: metric telemetry:metric:{plane}:{artifact}[.{category}]:{measure}; trace telemetry:trace:{plane}:{artifact}[.{category}] (no measure); log optional telemetry:log:...; event telemetry:event:... or Title Case analytics name.
Prefer:
- Measures: nouns (entity) count|size|age|staleness|freshness; past-tense verbs (event) latency|duration|throughput|error_rate|success_rate.
- Planes: ui, ux, be, db, nw, st, tm, sc, au, fn, if.
Avoid:
- Metrics not derived from 'then' quantifiable expectations, or events not derived from 'when' user actions (both MUST).
- Operations/wagons in the URN: they MUST go in attributes.
- A resource that is not a past-tense verb from the lexicon (MUST).
- A measure on a trace; a measure mismatched to artifact kind; a plane/measure outside the lists.
- An acceptance with no signals.
Why: Artifact-based URNs give every acceptance verifiable, uniformly named telemetry; operations/wagons in attributes keep URNs stable and low-cardinality.
