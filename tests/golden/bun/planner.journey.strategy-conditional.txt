Title: Select between declared train variants with guards in the interlocking
Given: Work involves mutually selected alternatives (workload classes, cache hit/miss, fallbacks, recovery paths or similar); current interlocking route resolution and journey continuations support this.
When: The alternatives are declared or changed.
Then: Select among declared linear train variants using the interlocking's deterministic guards and priorities.
Prefer:
- Declare a shortcut as its own route.
- Plan tests for matching and nonmatching guards, ambiguity/priority resolution, errors and contract preservation.
- Give every branch its own declared guard, train and accepted artifacts (required for each branch, not optional).
Avoid:
- Undeclared bypasses.
- Ambiguous guard matching without new acceptance.
- Changed observable behavior without new acceptance.
- Inferring unimplemented orchestration behavior from this pattern, or treating it as a new runtime dispatch option.
- A branch missing a declared guard, train or accepted artifacts.
Why: Current interlocking route resolution and journey continuations support this pattern; unimplemented orchestration behavior cannot be inferred from a pattern.
