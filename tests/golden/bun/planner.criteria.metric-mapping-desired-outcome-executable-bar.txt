Title: Give each mapped-pair WMBT an executable bar on its table metric
Given: A WMBT under plan/ has a dimension+direction that the dimension->metric table maps to a metric_id.
When: You create or edit that WMBT or its acceptances, or the SMOKE corpus check inspects it (unchanged WMBTs included).
Then: An acceptance states an executable bar: signal.metric equal to the table's metric_id for that pair, plus a signal.threshold that can be evaluated.
Prefer:
- Table, down (minimize|decrease) / up (maximize|increase), all metric:-prefixed: time latency_p50/latency_increase; effort user_steps/unmapped; likelihood error_ratio/success_ratio; frequency occurrence_rate/unmapped; quantity scrap_rate/throughput; financial value cost_per_unit/net_margin.
- Use the schema's exact dimension/direction spelling; minimize=decrease and maximize=increase resolve to the same metric_id.
- Copy the metric_id verbatim, metric: prefix included.
Avoid:
- A mapped-pair WMBT with no bar, a metric other than the table's, a threshold that cannot be evaluated, or a bar dropped on edit.
- Reading 'advisory' as permission never to add the bar — the debt is paid deliberately, in phases.
Why: A dimension+direction is testable only once it names a metric and an evaluable threshold; of 368 mapped WMBTs, none did.
