Title: Size a wagon on two axes: footprint and separability
Given: A wagon is being designed, sized or recomposed.
When: The planner decides the wagon's size and boundary.
Then: Size the wagon on two orthogonal axes: SIZE (intrinsic footprint: how much work is inside) and SEPARABILITY (is the wagon a well-separated unit in the produce/consume graph, i.e. is the cut correct?). Treat complexity as super-linear in cross-wagon coupling, not additive in component count.
Prefer:
- Size axis: the additive footprint, sum(count x weight), from planner.feature.footprint-scoring.
- Separability axis: a wagon is separable when its cohesion (shared-artifact / object-of-control density of its own WMBTs) is at least its tightest coupling (shared produce/consume artifact-name weight) to any single neighbor wagon.
Avoid:
- Sizing a wagon on footprint alone.
- Treating complexity as additive in component count.
Why: Complexity is super-linear in cross-wagon coupling, so footprint alone cannot tell you whether a cut is correct.
