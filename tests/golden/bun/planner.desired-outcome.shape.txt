Title: Give every WMBT entry an id, a statement and its proving artifacts
Given: A wagon decomposition containing What-Must-Be-True (WMBT) entries that you are authoring, editing, inspecting or validating.
When: You add, edit, review or validate any WMBT entry.
Then: Every WMBT entry declares its id, its statement, and the proving artifacts (the acceptances/tests that prove the statement).
Prefer:
- Write the id, the statement and the list of proving acceptances/tests together when the entry is created.
- When reviewing existing entries, flag or complete any entry lacking id, statement or proving artifacts.
Avoid:
- A WMBT entry missing its id, its statement, or its proving artifacts.
- Listing proving artifacts that do not actually prove the entry's statement.
Why: Downstream planning (per-WMBT acceptance coverage, feature grouping, smoke acceptances) operates over enumerated, well-shaped WMBTs; an entry without id, statement or proof cannot be referenced or verified.
