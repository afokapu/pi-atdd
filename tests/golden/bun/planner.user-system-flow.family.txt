Title: Declare each train's family (behavior or delivery) to match its terminal contract
Given: A train definition exists or is being written: it is authored, edited (family, terminal contract or train ID added, changed or removed), or reviewed/validated even if unchanged.
When: The planner sets, adds or corrects a train's family; adds, changes or removes its terminal contract; proposes or changes a train ID; or reviews/validates existing train definitions.
Then: Declare family as delivery if and only if the declared terminal contract requires a durable Acceptance Authority commit and terminates in platform:acceptance:commit-receipt; otherwise declare behavior.
Prefer:
- Derive family from the terminal contract (the source of truth); family is its validator-checked assertion, so re-derive it whenever the terminal contract changes.
- behavior: observe, exercise, classify, validate, recommend or report a capability against a system under examination; no authority commit.
- delivery: govern accepted-system state through a durable Acceptance Authority commit.
Avoid:
- Encoding family in the train ID (never an ID digit).
- Omitting family (mandatory) or treating it as a second source of truth that diverges from the terminal contract.
- Inferring family from the variant category (0-3, planner.train.categories) or vice versa; the axes are orthogonal.
Why: Behavior trains only exercise a capability; delivery trains change accepted-system state via an authority commit. Deriving family from the terminal contract keeps this checkable and out of IDs and variant categories.
