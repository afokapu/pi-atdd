Title: Use the shared meaning of WMBT (advisory semantic anchor)
Given: The term WMBT (What Must Be True) is used, asked about, or authored, in any context
When: you need its meaning
Then: Use this one meaning: a falsifiable statement of a single outcome that must hold for an artifact — scoped to one object of control along one dimension, direction, and lens — declared with an id and a statement, and paired with the acceptances that prove it, including at least one smoke acceptance.
Prefer:
- For the enforceable parts, follow the referenced rules: planner.wmbt.shape (id, statement, proving artifacts), planner.wmbt.object-of-control, planner.wmbt.dimension-/direction-/lens-selection and planner.wmbt.splitting-rules (single axis), planner.wmbt.statement-template, planner.wmbt.must-have-smoke-acceptance.
Avoid:
- Using WMBT for an unfalsifiable or multi-outcome statement.
- Treating this anchor as a validator; compliance is checked through the enforceable nodes that require this anchor.
Why: A WMBT needs one stable meaning so every WMBT rule checks the same concept. This anchor is advisory: it enforces nothing directly and is transitively protected by the rule nodes that require it.
