Title: Split any feature exceeding 2 new tables, 2 async jobs, or 2 integrations
Given: A feature whose scope includes tables, async jobs, or integrations
When: The planner authors, revises, or reviews the feature, or finds during any phase that the feature is over-limit
Then: If the feature has more than 2 new tables, more than 2 async jobs, or more than 2 integrations, split it so that every resulting feature stays within all three limits.
Prefer:
- Split along the excess. Move the extra tables, jobs, or integrations into a separate feature with its own acceptance.
Avoid:
- Leaving a feature that exceeds any one limit unsplit, or treating the limits as advisory.
- Gaming the count, e.g. merging distinct jobs or tables into one, or wrapping several dependencies behind one facade.
- Excluding existing jobs or integrations from the count. Only tables are limited to new ones.
Why: Tables bring maintenance, schema-evolution, and performance cost. Async jobs bring operational complexity (monitoring, retry, inconsistency). Integrations bring external dependencies, security exposure, and failures you cannot control.
