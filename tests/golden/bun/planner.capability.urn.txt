Title: Give every wagon a declared, stable URN and the plan/<wagon-slug>/ layout
Given: The change creates or modifies a wagon, meaning its defining manifest or its own file set, wherever it is located. Files that merely mention a wagon do not count.
When: The planner creates, edits, renames, moves or reorganises that wagon.
Then: The wagon declares a stable URN (wagon_urn), and that URN does not change when the wagon is edited, renamed or moved.
Prefer:
- Keep a wagon's existing URN when you edit, rename or move it.
- Give a new wagon a URN in the grammar defined by planner.wagon.urn-naming.
- Place every wagon under plan/<wagon-slug>/ with the documented file layout (wagon_layout). This is required by the same source statement. Derive the slug as planner.wagon.logical-vs-filesystem-naming specifies.
Avoid:
- A wagon with no declared URN, or with a URN derived from something that changes over time.
- Rewriting a wagon's URN as part of other edits.
- A wagon that lives outside plan/<wagon-slug>/, or whose file set departs from the documented layout.
Why: Trains and components refer to a wagon by its URN, and the URN must resolve to a real wagon at a predictable location. A missing or changing URN, or a misplaced wagon, breaks those references.
