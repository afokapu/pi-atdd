Title: Author nothing before the operator ratifies the locked decomposition
Given: A plan session whose decomposition the operator has not yet ratified and locked (the Ratify gate / confirm_boundary: where LLM/operator judgment ends and deterministic authoring begins).
When: Any atdd author <kind> writer would be invoked for a unit of the decomposition.
Then: Author nothing until the operator confirms and the decomposition is locked.
Prefer:
- On ratification, let atdd plan (the system, not the agent) deterministically invoke atdd author <kind> per locked unit: complete, atomic, idempotently replayable.
- In the session machine, keep the lock guard as the first statement of PlanSession.author(): raise SessionGateError when self.locked is False, before calling any author writer.
Avoid:
- Invoking an atdd author writer yourself, before or after ratification.
- An author pass that invokes any writer while the session is unlocked, or a guard moved after the first writer call.
Why: Ratify is the conversational-to-deterministic boundary; authoring only from an operator-locked decomposition keeps it complete, atomic and replayable rather than driven by unconfirmed agent judgment.
