Title: Plan each component with at most 5 methods; split it when more are needed
Given: A plan, wagon or component spec you are authoring, revising or reviewing declares a component and its methods (new or existing)
When: You plan or review any component, whatever the edit — adding, removing or leaving its methods unchanged
Then: Keep every component at 5 methods or fewer; if the capability needs more methods, split it into several focused components instead of exceeding the cap.
Prefer:
- Split an over-full component along business-capability lines so each resulting component owns one capability with ≤ 5 methods
- Keep a component small and focused, e.g. a validator with 3 methods (validate, eligibility check, explain)
Avoid:
- A single component with 9 (or any > 5) methods — split it into focused components
- Leaving an existing over-limit component as-is, or trimming it to a count still above 5 (e.g. 9 → 6), and treating that as compliant
- Gaming the cap by merging distinct operations into one catch-all method or moving methods into an unnamed helper bag instead of splitting by capability
Why: Capping a component at 5 methods keeps it focused on a single business capability; exceeding it signals the component is doing too much and should be split.
