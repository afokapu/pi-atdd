Title: Assess every route category of an interlocking
Given: An interlocking in the plan; its route space is the closed category set nominal, alternate, error, exception
When: The plan is assessed at CONFIRM (every interlocking, changed or not)
Then: Assess every category explicitly: nominal carries one or more routes, and alternate, error and exception each carry routes or a typed not-applicable basis from the closed vocabulary.
Prefer:
- For alternate, error and exception, state what happens: write the routes or pick the matching closed-vocabulary basis
- Ensure that, as assessed at CONFIRM, no category carries both routes and a not-applicable basis
Avoid:
- Silently omitting any category
- One token alternate route while error and exception stay unwritten
- Declaring nominal not-applicable (it is the executed path)
- Free-prose or copy-pasted not-applicable justification
- A not-applicable basis on a category that carries routes (stale contradiction)
Why: 'At least one non-nominal route' is gamed by a token alternate; assessing each category forces stating what happens on each, and a typed not-applicable stops the escape eroding into copy-pasted prose.
