Title: Place each declared contract schema file at the path its artifact identity spells out
Given: A plan declares a produced artifact with an identity (theme:segment[:...][.segment]) and a concrete contract .schema.json file. This holds whether the declaration is new, edited (including identity-only renames) or unchanged.
When: You author, edit or confirm a plan that contains such a declaration.
Then: Make the contract path mirror the identity exactly: every ':' and '.' boundary becomes a directory boundary under contracts/, and the leaf is the <aspect>.schema.json file (contracts/{theme}/.../{aspect}.schema.json).
Prefer:
- Derive the path mechanically: split the identity on ':' and '.', turn the segments into directories under contracts/, and put the .schema.json leaf last.
- When you rename an identity, update the contract path in the same edit (and the reverse).
Avoid:
- Putting a .schema.json outside contracts/, or under a directory that does not match an identity segment (an extra, missing or renamed folder). A mis-located .schema.json is a violation.
- Picking a convenient or grouped folder layout that breaks the identity→path mapping.
- Treating an existing, unchanged mis-pathed contract as out of scope.
Unless:
- The artifact's contract is null. Out of scope.
- The contract's physical form is a directory reference, not a .schema.json file. Out of scope.
Why: When the path is derived from the identity, a contract file can be found from its identity without guessing. A mis-located schema blocks plan confirmation.
