Title: Declare the fake contract on hermetic acceptances that use fakes
Given: An acceptance with execution_kind: hermetic_integration and non-empty permitted_fakes
When: the acceptance exists or is written
Then: Declare the full hermetic fake-contract block — exercised_boundaries, permitted_fakes, one fake_contract_fidelity entry {name, fidelity, known_gaps} per permitted fake, and live_smoke_required; the declaration IS the contract.
Prefer:
- exercised_boundaries uses only boundary_kind vocabulary values for surfaces actually exercised.
- Each permitted fake gets exactly one fake_contract_fidelity entry with matching name, fidelity and a known_gaps list.
- live_smoke_required: true when a paired execution_kind: live_smoke sibling must exist under the same WMBT; optionally add live_smoke_reason.
Avoid:
- A permitted fake without a fidelity entry, or an entry missing name, fidelity or known_gaps.
- Omitting exercised_boundaries or live_smoke_required (always required).
Unless:
- permitted_fakes is empty — the fidelity rule does not apply.
Why: The declaration IS the contract: for each fake substituted for live infra it records name, fidelity and known gaps.
