Title: List only declared wagons in a train
Given: A non-excluded plan contains a train whose `wagons:` list names wagon slugs (changed or not).
When: The plan is authored, edited or checked — e.g. a train's `wagons:` list is written, a wagon declaration is renamed/removed, or existing plans are validated.
Then: Every wagon the train lists under `wagons:` is declared as `wagon:<slug>`.
Prefer:
- Declare the missing wagon as `wagon:<slug>`.
- Or remove the wagon from the train.
- When renaming or removing a wagon declaration, update every train that lists it in the same change.
Avoid:
- Listing a wagon in a train that has no `wagon:<slug>` declaration — a train ordering a wagon that does not exist cannot run.
- Declaring an empty placeholder wagon solely to silence the check instead of planning the wagon or removing it from the train.
Unless:
- Vendored and generated trees (`node_modules`, `dist`, `build`, `_generated`) are never scanned.
Why: A train ordering a wagon that does not exist cannot run. Wagon membership is the train-specific case of reference resolution.
