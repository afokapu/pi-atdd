Title: Write the contract URN as contract:{artifact_name}, copied verbatim
Given: A contract URN for an artifact appears anywhere it is authored or checked (e.g. a plan, wagon, WMBT, acceptance or doc), whether new or already existing
When: that contract URN is written, edited, or validated
Then: The URN is exactly contract:{artifact_name}, with the artifact name copied verbatim, preserving every colon and dot.
Prefer:
- Copy the artifact name as-is after the contract: prefix, e.g. commons:identifiers.uuid -> contract:commons:identifiers.uuid.
- Keep multi-segment names intact, e.g. commons:ux:foundations:color.primary -> contract:commons:ux:foundations:color.primary.
Avoid:
- Normalising separators (turning colons into dots/slashes or dots into colons) inside the URN.
- Shortening, re-casing, dropping or adding segments so the URN no longer equals the artifact name.
- Inventing a URN form other than contract:{artifact_name}.
Why: The contract URN is the URN form of the artifact name; an exact mirror keeps one artifact identity that references and the schema $id can share without translation.
