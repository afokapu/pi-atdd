Title: Use a colon for hierarchical descent in artifact names
Given: A planner is composing, renaming or reviewing an artifact identifier whose segments span different conceptual levels of the resource tree
When: choosing or checking the separator where the identifier descends from a more general level to a more specific one
Then: Use a colon for that hierarchical descent: join the general level and the more specific level beneath it with a colon (general->specific, navigating deeper through organizational subsystems).
Prefer:
- Order segments from general to specific, joined by colons, e.g. commons:ux:foundations:color (theme->subsystem->category->item).
- Use colons for organizational subsystems and collections as you navigate deeper, e.g. scenario:catalog:primitives:actor (domain->collection->type->entity).
Avoid:
- Using a colon between parts that sit on the same conceptual level, which falsely signals descent.
- Using another separator (dot, dash, underscore) to step down a level, which hides the hierarchy.
- Ordering segments specific->general under colons.
Why: Hierarchical descent means moving from general to specific down the resource tree, and that movement is marked by a colon.
