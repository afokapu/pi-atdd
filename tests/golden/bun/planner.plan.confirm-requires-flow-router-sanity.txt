Title: Do not ratify a train-bearing plan whose interlocking sanity fails
Given: A train-bearing plan session at the CONFIRM phase where a required interlocking sanity check fails
When: The planner attempts to ratify (lock) the plan
Then: Ratify must refuse to lock the plan, leaving the session unlocked.
Prefer:
- Read the failed rule for the reported train, interlocking and route, fix the plan's interlocking, and re-run ratify.
Avoid:
- Marking the session locked/confirmed, even partially, while it is train-bearing and a required interlocking sanity check fails.
- Making sanity pass by dropping the train or its routes from the plan instead of fixing the interlocking.
Why: Locking a train-bearing plan whose interlocking is unsound would ratify routes that cannot run safely; confirm is the last gate before the plan is fixed.
