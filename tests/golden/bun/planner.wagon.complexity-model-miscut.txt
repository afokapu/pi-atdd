Title: A small wagon can still be mis-cut: MERGE or SPLIT
Given: A wagon cut is being judged.
When: The wagon is densely coupled to a neighbor, or it contains two internal clusters.
Then: The wagon can be mis-cut even if its footprint is small. Densely coupled to a neighbor -> MERGE. Two internal clusters -> SPLIT.
Prefer:
- Apply the source's two patterns as stated: densely coupled to a neighbor -> MERGE; two internal clusters -> SPLIT.
Avoid:
- Calling a wagon well-cut just because its footprint is small.
Why: Complexity is super-linear in cross-wagon coupling, so a small wagon can still be mis-cut.
