Title: Give each artifact one producing wagon; consumers reference its contract
Given: A plan declares wagon interfaces with produce[] and consume[] artifact entries
When: The planner adds or changes a produce[] or consume[] entry for an artifact
Then: Declare the artifact in produce[] of exactly one wagon, which generates (owns) its contract; every other wagon using it declares it in consume[] and references that existing contract instead of generating its own.
Prefer:
- Producer entry: artifact name, destination, and (optionally) the path of the contract it generates
- Consumer entry: same artifact name, `from` the producing wagon, and (if given) the producer's contract path
- Contract field is optional on every entry but recommended; new wagons should add explicit contract references
Avoid:
- Two wagons declaring the same artifact in produce[] (split ownership)
- A consumer generating or redefining its own contract for an artifact another wagon produces
- Consuming an artifact no wagon produces (orphan)
Unless:
- Any entry may omit the contract field (required=false, backward compatibility); existing wagons work with implicit contracts
Why: Single producer ownership with consumers referencing the same contract makes bidirectional producer/consumer coverage and orphan detection possible.
