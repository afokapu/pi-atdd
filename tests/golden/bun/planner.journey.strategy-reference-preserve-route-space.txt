Title: Keep the full route space when planning reference execution
Given: A plan follows a journey's declared routes and continuations.
When: You write its plan, wagons, WMBTs or acceptances.
Then: Preserve guards, error/exception routes, artifacts, terminal outcomes, side effects and ordering, not only the nominal path.
Prefer:
- Use existing journey and acceptance tests to prove behavior.
Avoid:
- Planning only the nominal path.
Why: A nominal path is not the full route space. Following declared routes does not by itself guarantee these properties; the plan has to keep them.
