Title: Pick exactly one optimization dimension per WMBT
Given: A WMBT is being authored or revised from a user's intent
When: the planner states what the user wants to optimize
Then: Select exactly one optimization dimension (the core metric the user wants to optimize) from: Time, Effort, Likelihood, Frequency, Quantity/Amount, Financial Value.
Prefer:
- Match the user's intent to a dimension via its keywords; decompose into dimension/actor/action/object using the description and rationale.
- Time = duration/speed/waiting/delay/rework (performer's most finite resource); Effort = physical or cognitive load (muscular force or concentration/learning/decision-making); Likelihood = probability of an outcome (predictability, control, success vs error).
- Frequency = rate of occurrence (reduce repetitive/non-value-added tasks); Quantity/Amount = volume of a resource consumed or produced (optimize inputs/outputs); Financial Value = monetary impact (cost, revenue, opportunity cost).
- Shape like: 'Time: minimize onboarding time', 'Likelihood: increase usable experiment results', 'Effort: reduce mental effort to find a bug'.
Avoid:
- Assigning two or more dimensions to one WMBT; split the WMBT instead.
- Using a dimension outside the six-value catalog.
Why: One dimension names the core metric to optimize; statement templates and acceptance metrics derive from it, so mixing breaks that mapping.
