Title: Cached or incremental results: define reuse-safety contracts
Given: Work includes an expensive, repeatable read or derivation with stable identities and tractable dependencies
When: deciding to reuse its results or recompute only affected portions
Then: Reuse valid results, or recompute only affected portions, only where you define cache key, freshness, invalidation, authorization, tenant isolation and side-effect contracts that make reuse safe; a hit must never omit a required permission check or mutation.
Prefer:
- Reuse only where freshness and invalidation contracts make it safe.
Avoid:
- A cache hit that skips a required permission check or mutation.
Why: Unsafe reuse serves stale, cross-tenant or unauthorized results and silently skips required mutations.
