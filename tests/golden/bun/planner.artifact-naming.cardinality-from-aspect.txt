Title: Derive artifact cardinality from the aspect's plurality
Given: An artifact name of colon-separated segments ending in an aspect, optionally preceded by category segments (categories are not required, e.g. scenario:fragment) and optionally followed by a variant.
When: The artifact's cardinality (one item vs collection) is determined or interpreted, whether for a new name or an existing one.
Then: Take cardinality from the ASPECT (the final noun before any variant) alone: plural aspect → collection/array schema or directory of items; singular aspect → single-item schema.
Prefer:
- Identify the aspect first (final noun before the variant), then read its plurality to pick or interpret the shape.
- Pluralize the aspect when you mean a collection (scenario:fragments → collection); keep it singular for one item (scenario:fragment → one item).
Avoid:
- Inferring cardinality from a category segment: commons:ux:foundations:color is ONE item even though 'foundations' is plural; commons:ux:foundations:colors is an array of colors.
- Letting the variant segment change cardinality.
- Declaring an array/directory under a singular aspect, or a single-item schema under a plural aspect.
Why: Cardinality (single item vs collection) is decided by the aspect's plurality, so the name alone indicates one item or a collection; categories and variants do not affect cardinality.
