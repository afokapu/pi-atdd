Title: Build every new component URN with utils.graph.URNBuilder.component
Given: A component URN is created, changed, or reused
When: you produce or declare it
Then: Build it via utils.graph.URNBuilder.component(wagon_id, feature_id, component_name, side, layer). Never hand-concatenate it. A reused URN must be one that was built this way.
Avoid:
- Hand-typing or string-concatenating a new component URN.
Why: A single builder gives the graph one stable URN form.
