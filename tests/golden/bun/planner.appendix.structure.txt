Title: Order wagon appendix headings by the documented heading order
Given: A wagon document has, or is gaining, appendix sections (supplementary sections of the wagon document).
When: Any part of that wagon document is authored or edited — appendix sections, main-body headings/anchors, or unrelated body content.
Then: The appendix headings follow the documented heading order.
Prefer:
- Take the heading sequence from the documented heading order for wagon appendices rather than inventing one per document; if that documentation cannot be located, report the gap instead of choosing an order.
- Keep the companion obligation (see split suggestion): every appendix section links back to the main wagon body — write each back-link as you write the section.
- After any edit to the document, especially renaming or moving a main-body heading, re-check that every appendix back-link still resolves.
Avoid:
- Appendix headings in ad-hoc or per-author order.
- Any appendix section without a working link back to the main wagon body (orphaned supplementary content, or a back-link broken by a main-body edit).
Why: A fixed appendix heading order and back-links keep large wagon documents navigable and auditable.
