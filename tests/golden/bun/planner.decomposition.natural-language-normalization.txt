Title: Normalize operator replies to keep/pivot/kill and confirm unclear intent
Given: During decomposition the operator replies to a keep/pivot/kill decision, in prose or in decision words, and the reply needs interpretation.
When: The planner records the operator's decision and proceeds.
Then: Normalize the reply to exactly one of keep, pivot or kill, and for pivot name the modification the operator asked for.
Prefer:
- Accept free-text replies; map them onto keep/pivot/kill instead of demanding the operator retype a keyword.
- Whenever the interpretation is ambiguous, whatever the reply format, restate your interpretation (the normalized decision, plus the named modification for pivot) and get the operator's confirmation before acting on it.
Avoid:
- Proceeding on an ambiguous interpretation of the operator's reply without restating it and confirming first.
- Recording a pivot without naming the modification it implies.
Why: The operator may answer in prose, so the reply must be normalized to keep/pivot/kill to be recorded as a decision; restating and confirming unclear intent avoids acting on a misread interpretation.
