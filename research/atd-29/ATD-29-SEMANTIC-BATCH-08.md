## Cross-source state separation and evidence boundaries

- `PA-P-INT01` (identity present) and `PA-P-INT03` (identity unique) are distinct; a complete identity field can still duplicate another artifact. `PA-P-INT04` (parse), `PA-P-INT06` (schema), and `PA-P-INT05` (reference resolution) vary independently, so a syntactically valid YAML file cannot be counted as a valid graph join.
- Journey `PA-P-JNY01` route closure requires **exactly one** continuation or terminal per reachable route; `PA-P-JNY02` checks no declared flow-router remains unreachable. A plan with no journey may pass closure vacuously and still violate composition; do not merge these states.
- Strategy-only source rules describe choices and constraints for optimization. Most do **not** assert current executable schedules or measurable artifacts; especially concurrency, pipelining and speculation lack a generic scheduler. Their NO_CANDIDATE decisions retain all authored guidance, no automatic runtime admission or waiver. The existing `PA-P-JNY01` covers the real declared conditional route space; no additional strategy-shaped OoC is manufactured.
- `PA-P-TEL01` accepted requirement/exception decision, `PA-P-TEL02` producer ownership, `PA-P-TEL03` metric-label safety and `PA-P-TEL04` tracking item identity/shape can fail independently. A NO_OP before adoption is not successful telemetry governance and not a blanket exemption.
- `PA-P-PLAN01` phase lock, `PA-P-PLAN02` local issue binding, `PA-P-PLAN03` route sanity, `PA-P-PLAN04` granularity visibility and `PA-P-PLAN05` hand-authored unit schema are separate gate subjects. Operator-ratified state is not permission to self-admit engineering OoCs.
- `PA-P-THM01` membership and `PA-P-THM02` reserved commons floor are distinct; a theme can be registered while produced artifact identity prefix mismatches `PA-P-THM03`.
