## Cross-source reconciliation and meaningful distinctions

- The `PA-P-DEC05` operator-decision standing is **not** authored plan artifact correctness; a kept heuristic/main job does not create a file. The completeness of source reading (`PA-P-DEC02`) is separate from whether its derived job statement uses JTBD (`PA-P-DEC04`).
- Documentation is not one vague “quality” subject: the declaration `PA-P-DOC03`, identity uniqueness `PA-P-DOC05`, graph target closure `PA-P-DOC06`, currency `PA-P-DOC07`, derived journey view `PA-P-DOC08`, and deterministic render `PA-P-DOC09` vary independently. A `COULD_NOT_CHECK` toolchain gap must not be promoted to `NOT_APPLICABLE` or PASS.
- Feature feedback-loop completion `PA-P-FEA04` is a **three-stage** observable state: producer detected/acted, consumer reacted, and predicate stopped firing. Presence of `kind: feedback-loop` alone establishes only declared applicability. Docs-only suppression has its own standing `PA-P-FEA05`, not an auto-waiver.
- Feature size `PA-P-FEA09`, table/jobs/integration hard limits `PA-P-FEA08`, weighted footprint `PA-P-FEA06`, and semantic grouping `PA-P-FEA07` are independently varying; low numeric size is insufficient to prove good semantic cohesion.
- Two source entries represent **vocabulary/reference procedures** rather than new OoCs: `planner.decomposition.canonical-steps` and `planner.feature.verb-selection-by-artifact-type`. `planner.feature.estimation` is a drafting cue table; scored footprint state is evidenced by the separate footprint source, so no duplicate candidate is invented.
