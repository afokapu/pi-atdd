## Duplicate/composite reconciliation and state-boundary tests

- `PA-P-ACC01` human-readable Given/When/Then completeness, `PA-P-ACC07` route statement, `PA-P-ACC08` telemetry signal ID, and `PA-P-ACC03` harness selection are **independently changing** even when all belong to one acceptance. A syntactically valid harness name never proves the route or a passing test.
- `PA-P-ACC05` fake fidelity is separate from the live-versus-hermetic environment state `PA-P-ACC04`. A recorded cassette can be truthful and still provide no live-infrastructure confidence.
- `PA-P-OUT01` is a proposed measure of **desired-outcome document semantic coherence**, not the real world's improved Time/Effort/Likelihood state. Desired change **direction** is an intent attribute and does not itself satisfy an OoC criterion. The acceptance cap `PA-P-OUT03` and smoke declaration coverage `PA-P-OUT04` vary independently of semantic coherence.
- `PA-P-ACC06` deliberately retains two identity surfaces: historical `identity.id` and canonical `identity.urn`. Their grammar differs; no automatic equivalence can be assumed without a grounded join.
- `planner.desired-outcome.object-of-control` is **NO_CANDIDATE** in this Pass A because the source instructs synthesis of a kebab noun phrase without evidence of a reusable engineering state, measurement path or owner admission. This is a critical negative case, not a disparagement of its planning value.
- All authoring prompt/lookup rules marked NO_CANDIDATE are kept as **normative instruction evidence** in the pinned source graph. They are not removed from the corpus or relabeled as valid source IDs. A negative candidate disposition is not a waiver of the convention.
