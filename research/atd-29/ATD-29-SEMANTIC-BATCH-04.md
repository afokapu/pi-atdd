## Cross-source identity and independently variable state boundaries

`PA-P-ART01` (name grammar and theme identity) is distinct from `PA-P-ART03` (physical file placement), `PA-P-ART02` (collection cardinality), and `PA-P-ART05` (contract schema identity/version): all may vary independently on the same domain artifact. `PA-P-ART04` represents migration-time reference coherence; a stable identifier by itself does not establish correct data transport. Appendix type/name `PA-P-APP01`, location `PA-P-APP02`, and document backlink `PA-P-APP03` are separate state aspects even when one attachment carries them all.

**Explicit semantic collision requiring reviewer:** `planner.artifact-naming.schema-identifier` states a contract schema's `$id` is the clean artifact identity without `urn:contract:` or version, whereas `planner.artifact-naming.validation-rules` includes a technical constraint spelling `$id` as `urn:contract:{artifact_name}`. Both statements are preserved as authored evidence. Their contradictory $id forms must not be reconciled by guessed precedence, nor repaired by this Pass A agent. The latter ledger row remains UNRESOLVED with a concrete reason.

**No-candidate control:** `planner.appendix.llm-guidelines` directs an agent's reading/prompting procedure. It does not alone define a durable measurable engineering state subject; a referenced appendix is a Resource instance, not an admitted OoC.

**Review boundary:** These state lenses require ATD-28 O1–O8 independent challenge and ATD-27 reconciliation. File-path compliance, identity syntax, and artifact existence do not prove an actual Desired Change Path, authorized actuator, or admitted OoC.
