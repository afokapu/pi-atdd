## Source collisions, doubles, and state variation

- `PA-B-TMT01` deliberately combines `tester.telemetry.captured-sink` with `tester.telemetry.emit`: both measure emission sink assertion. Exact emitted identity `PA-B-TMT02`, required-item coverage `PA-B-TMT03` and ordering `PA-B-TMT05` vary independently; a spy call does not prove a required item fired at the right time.
- Browser E2E binding `PA-B-E2E01`, route reachability `PA-B-E2E02`, and semantic user-path proof `PA-B-E2E05` are separate Resource aspects: a test can bind a valid plan ID but never reach the named route or assert the promised user-visible outcome.
- Flow-router test route coverage `PA-B-FRT02` and ordered executed flow `PA-B-FRT05` cannot be merged: a correctly selected branch may execute the wrong product capability sequence. Likewise trace binding `PA-B-FRT04` requires all seven authored fields, not only the selected flow ID.
- RED markers `PA-B-RED02` are only a static proxy; a meaningful counterfactual behavioral assertion `PA-B-RED01` must also fail without the implementation. SMOKE collaborator fidelity `PA-B-SMK01` and externally observable result `PA-B-SMK02` vary independently.
- **Explicit source contradiction:** `tester.test.no-self-skip`'s authored statement says `.failing` is disallowed, whereas its own `self_skip` term says `.failing` is deliberately excluded, and `tester.red.fails-first` accepts it as a RED marker. Its ledger status is **UNRESOLVED / concrete contradiction**. No active convention is changed and no silent precedence is guessed.
- `shared.profiles.presets` governs owner-approved **accumulating profile adoption**; selecting a preset is not automatic activation or permission to relax prior strict profiles. A source-only mount without tests is NO_OP for `tester.hypermedia.verb-endpoint-coverage`, *not* a denial of untested routes. An evidence record unavailable for reviewer inspection is COULD_NOT_CHECK and blocks, not PASS.

**Handoff boundary:** source-grounded provisional semantic candidate records only. Independent ATD-28 O1–O8, ATD-27 reconciliation and owner authorization remain mandatory. Graph reachability, a passing GitHub test, or a source-authored policy is not an admitted engineering OoC.
