## Identity and variation reconciliation

- `PA-P-CAP02` couples the two source rules that describe intrinsic footprint versus a separate coupling-complexity axis; their measured axes remain independent, with advisory thresholds not treated as strict. `PA-P-CAP07` cycle absence is a stronger enforced graph invariant than `PA-P-CAP09` separability advice. One valid SCC result does **not** prove a good cut.
- `PA-P-CAP03` semantic cohesion is independent of `PA-P-CAP08` artifact declaration syntax, `PA-P-CAP04` feature manifest coverage, and `PA-P-CAP05` identifier/path correspondence. A capability file can be syntactically complete and semantically incoherent.
- `PA-P-COMP01` component derivation from ioSeeds and artifacts, `PA-P-COMP04` event interface alignment, `PA-P-COMP05` layer consistency and `PA-P-COMP06` method bound are separate measured aspects. Count-limit sources do not authorize arbitrary decomposition.
- `PA-P-COV01` and `PA-P-COV02` are bidirectional relations between different bearer classes; `PA-P-COV03` captures the larger hierarchy and its inherited evidence but is **not** proof that every edge is implemented and tested.
- **Source contradiction**: `planner.component.structure` specifies a different punctuation pattern for component URNs from `planner.component.urn`'s six colon-separated canonical identity. The structure row remains UNRESOLVED, with both source references preserved; no invented precedence or source rewrite.
- **Negative controls**: `planner.capability.refinement-extraction`, `planner.capability.slug-dirname-conversion`, and three `planner.criteria` lookup/scaffold rules are procedures/templates rather than independent state subjects. Their authored obligations remain active; negative OoC extraction is not a waiver.
